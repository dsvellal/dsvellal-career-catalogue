Comments :
Datta played an important role in transforming the IT E&S team.
Prior to this year, the IT team was focused on building pipelines
as part of the PDP, an endeavor that was not aligned toward
GitHub InnerSource. Now, under Datta's guidance, the team is
productively creating not only usable, but reusable workflows,
and then incorporating them into the SaaS Factory Pipelines, all
on GitHub InnerSource for the benefit and use of anyone in the
company. Also prior to six months ago, it was a manually
intensive exercise for a development team to adopt the Security
CoE's required Fortify tool. He similarly showed them the power
of Team Topologies, whereby Platform teams ultimately serve
End-to-End teams by providing easy-to-use, self-service APIs to
leverage their services. He demonstrated how to create a
reusable workflow in GitHub such that a team can wire their
project to Fortify in a self-service manner with a few mouse clicks.
What used to take months (from my own personal experience
wiring my former project to Fortify) now takes minutes. This
wasn't just a "task"; this was a vision, an implementation of the
vision, and a successful selling of the vision to teams that needed
it to be sold to them for their own success. He has succeeded in
teaching them the mindset; the other teams "get it" now, and
actively create numerous resuable workflows for the benefit of all.
Next year, I hope that Datta can not only continue to develop the
GitHub InnerSource environment and migrate teams to it, but
also more generally re-establish himself as an important
".connect" and software quality leader within the SW CoE.

Comments :
Overall, this year has been very revealing. The theme of the year
was SaaS factory and DevOps. Although there were moments of
chaos in the first half of the year, I chose to pick up an area that
gives me clarity and stability in work and was able to make
progress. I would be happy to build on the work done and continue
this to realize the value it brings to the DevOps ecosystem of
Philips.
