What's in this report
Your Philips 360 feedback report provides you with a comprehensive breakdown of your behaviors including:
How to interpret this report: Outlines how to interpret your feedback, including the rating scales and raters
responded.
Circumplex summary: The circumplex summary is designed to give you a high level summary of your
feedback received.
Detailed competency breakdowns: Each section breaks each competency down into its individual
statements by rater category.
Highest and lowest rated statements: Your core strengths and development opportunities.
Unknown strengths and unknown development areas: Areas where you perceive yourself as significantly
more/less capable than others perceive you.
Comments: In this area you will find the additional comments your raters were asked to provide.

How to interpret this report

This report is designed to provide you with rich insights into your strengths and development areas as
perceived by the people you work with. When reading your results:
AVG refers to the average of all others, excluding self. The average is shown per behavioral
statement (ie each row in the table), and for the competency overall (in the blue row at the top
of the table).
When there is more than one individual in a rater category, the corresponding column for that
rater category shows the average per behavioral statement/descriptor, and for the competency
overall (in the blue row at the top of the table).
For all raters other than self and (secondary) manager, there is an anonymity threshold of 3. If an
AVG is displayed for a rater group, it indicates that this threshold has been met. If less than 3
people respond, the responses are included in the AVG column which represents all raters other
than self.
Norms
For the Philips behaviors, your report may include a company average score in the norms
section for each competency. This is the average score for the behavior overall, of everyone in
Philips who has received 360-degree feedback against the behavior at the same level. The
company average will be reviewed regularly and updated, which means that it could change
slightly on your report, if you decide to print the report over a longer period of time. The
company average helps you to understand how closely you are aligned with others who have
previously taken the 360 feedback survey within Philips, which may assist you to prioritize your
development focus areas and continue to utilize your areas of strength. The difference between
your AVG score for the competency and the company average score will also be shown.If your
average is higher than the company average it will be a positive number; if lower, it will be a
negative number.
If you have competencies in your report in addition to the Philips behaviors, you may have a
Universal and/or Digital Ready Organization benchmark. The benchmark figures reported on the
Philips 360 reports provide a way to compare the results of Philips leaders to other organizations
around the world who have done the same exercise with DDI, our 360 survey partner.
Benchmark figures will be included only for those competencies where DDI has sufficient data
samples to create a significant comparison point. Philips leaders are compared to high
performance scores, to reflect our position as a market leader.
The Universal Benchmark is comprised of DDI competency ratings from over 650 organizations
globally. Data is cross-industry, global and incorporates over five million individual scores.
The Digital Ready Organization benchmark is comprised of organizations whose company
characteristics are aligned with high degrees of digital and technology progress. These companies
are largely characterized as operating within the technology or professional services sector
(including health), and have demonstrated high growth rates.
The difference between your AVG score for the competency and any norm in your report will be
shown. All organizations are unique, and the many differences can manifest in the scores leaders
receive in their 360 feedback. As such, this data is intended to be used just as another lens to use
when prioritizing your development focus areas.
