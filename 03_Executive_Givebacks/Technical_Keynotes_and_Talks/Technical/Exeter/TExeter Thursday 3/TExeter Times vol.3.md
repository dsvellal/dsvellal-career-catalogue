The Power of Collaboration
Man is a social being, so is the world he lives in. We have been, knowingly or unknowingly sharing and collaborating with others in
our day to day life. Take social media for example. Updates happen on the social media much before it reaches to anyone in person!
Another example is news – we have apps, readers, trends and many more tools on the social media that keeps us updated about the
“happening events” much before any newspaper or tabloid reports it! In today’s world, it’s all about knowing things and how we
apply this knowledge in our day-day work. This “updated-knowledge” clearly defines the best from the rest. Don’t be under the
impression that we have to “know-it-all” to be the best – collaboration helps us fill that gap! Here’s how we can leverage the power
of collaboration:


Jack of some, master of a few: Everyone can’t be good at everything. However, we can have friends who are good at some
things! This is the best possible example of collaboration where offering technical consults to friends/colleagues and
seeking technical consults from them helps us break down complex puzzles into simple manageable pieces and solve them
quickly and efficiently.



Eureka moments or the lack of it: Problem discovery and solution is a big challenge in the current world where we waste
time in finding solutions to puzzles not knowing that someone has solved a similar problem and that solution can be
adopted easily. Collaboration helps us in building our collective knowledge base there by gaining full control of the
problem in front of us.



How much does a panda weigh? Enough to break the ice!: Its strange but true, how often people from different cultural
background, technical expertise, diverse IT experience unite to solve a challenging puzzle, to realize that – When going gets
tough, the tough get going! Minds and heart unite towards achieving a single purpose and in that we find happiness!



You are known by the friends you keep: Every successful leader in this world is a great collaborator. He/she knows from
whom to get the right information, who to reach out to, to get things done and how to offer help so that others can leverage
their help and grow. Growth is always mutual and every successful leader understands and uses the support system that
collaboration offers to its fullest extent.



Division of Labor: One of the advantages of collaborative efforts in the workplace is the way that the work is divided.
When more than one person is involved in accomplishing a certain task, particularly when it is a large project, it helps for
everyone to have a small portion of the responsibility to ensure things get done versus loading one or two people with too
much work to accomplish the task.



Greater Creative Input: When we have different people collaborating on a project, we get a greater sense of creative input.
We are able to tap into the creative combination of several members in a group. The collection of different ideas,
approaches can spur innovative results that can in turn raise the visibility and quality of the outcome.

The most important thing to note is – we live in a world where success is also dependent on how well our work and ideas get
accepted and adopted by a large group of people. This means success rate is measured through increased sphere-of-influence and
reduced degree-of-separation; both of which is achieved only through active-collaboration. TExeter is our humble effort to
introduce each one of you to everyone else in the company, so that we can collectively build our technical acumen, tackle logical
puzzles and start taking on complex challenges by leveraging the power of collaboration.

I just Love this quote “You want something? Remember, you got way to go! So you better stop at NOTHING”.

end, it’s also plays an important role by grouping people, making them to have a good relationship with each
other and leads us towards a meaningful life.

1.

When did you start Blogging?
Have you heard of The Theory of Everything- The movie on Stephen Hawking? My first blog had nothing to do with relativity
but I had tried to explain Theory of Love (smiles). After a trip to Pondicherry, I still remember writing about 30-40pages of
what happened in those 3 days. Then in 3rd or 4th semester of engineering, I and my friend started writing tech blogs and that
went on till Iron was hot. Now here I am - My first blog for TExeter. If Google hasn't removed my content then you can find my
first blog here – http://sky-treasure.blogspot.in/2010/05/theory-of-love.html

2.

How do you manage to find time to pursue your hobbies?
When I was in high school my Social Studies teacher asked us a question- "What is that one thing that each of us have in equal?
Every child, every adult irrespective of who they are, where they were born, they all have it in the same amount. What's that?" There was
pin drop silence in the so called fish market! After a long pause and looking at our clueless faces, the teacher said, "Time"
Everyone in this world has got only 24hrs a day. How we use it depends on us and that's what defines us. And coming to the
main question, I don't find time to pursue my hobbies; whenever I feel like doing something, I Just do it! Oh! This line is catchy
might go into next Nike ads. (Smirks)

3.

What motivated you to contribute to TExeter?
It's like giving a chance to Indian Cricket team to play matches against Sri Lanka (cheeky smile) - always ready on their toes
(laughs). I like blogging and you guys gave me an opportunity to contribute to TExeter in the form of a blog and thank you for
that.

4.

What is your message to fellow colleagues?
I feel like Ctrl + C & Ving some quote from internet right now (laughs). Let me not do that! Okay, here's my thought- "There
are always 2 choices for everything, however hard the question is, it always ends up in either 0 or 1 [Binary]. So choose
wisely."

5.

How have you been benefited from TExeter?
Got a cap, t-shirt, water bottle, pen, mug and lots of chocolates (laughs) but fun part aside, TExeter Thursdays – which I always
look forward to, give us a chance to do something other than our routine work with the same skill set, which is remarkably
unique. I got to work with different people in different competitions, and made new friends. I am not that good at making new
friends, but TExeter did help me on that front. Joined the group with 4 new people for the recent contest and Voila! I made for
new friends. Thank you for that, TExeter.

How else can TExeter contribute/improve?
You guys are a doing a great job in keeping the curiosity level up and it's getting better and better with each event. The more
people participate, the better it gets. That's all I can say. It would be great if in future, we can see a TExeter Hackathon, with
predefined problem statements to solve and build a thing or two out of it.

7.

What do you think is the significance of collaboration in present times?
Collaboration is the future! Whoever coined the name WEB was a sheer genius. Everything is getting connected in one or the
other way, and web plays an important role in it. Gone are the days when a phone was meant to make calls and a watch was to
check time. Location Based Service, Augmented Reality, Internet of Things etc. is making it possible. You can see things
which are not there in front of you (I don’t mean Ghosts (smiles)) and communicate with people around the world and work
together towards common goal. At TExeter, we interact with people who have knowledge about different technologies. Now, if
I face problems in XML parsing or OPA - I run to Deepak, for SQL – to Abhishek, JAVA – to Abhijith, ADF –to Aditya,
Backbone – to Sumukh and for everything else we have Bharath (Laughs). This is one example of collaboration.
