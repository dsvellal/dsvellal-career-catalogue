The TExeter team wanted to know how you have embraced our programs and what
TExeter means to you. So we asked a few of our colleagues to give us candid responses
about what TExeter means. Here are some answers:

the team barrier, let there be a free flow of information facilitating faster and more

intended to double-up as an information sharing platform, where we encourage people to
read through the posts, comment on them, post your own questions, puzzles, problems,
situations, incidents anything that is related to technology. If you have not registered yet,
we highly encourage you to register and start participating. TExeter as a team will always
work towards collaboration and knowledge sharing.

What is TExeter’s TechBucket-Challenge?
We have all heard of the recently-gone-viral
Ice-bucket challenge. Wonder what’s a Tech
bucket challenge?? Well, we asked a few folks
through the forum and here are a few
interesting ideas:
“Give out a Syntax / Statement and let the
participant(s) do "match the following" with its
respective Tech Stack?” – Very nice!!
“Put assorted tech issues and throw at Poor
techies and make them rich with rewards & feed
some chocos to sweeten them if they solve ;) ” –
really funny and innovative!!
“Is it something like each person solves a
puzzle/riddle/tech question thrown at him and
later nominates three other people to solve the
same or new question!!” – Haha!! Good one
"Do we have to solve one question from a
bucket and proceed to the next bucket and solve
another question?? This continues until we
complete a set of buckets?" – Nice!!
“There will be multiple tech questions loaded in
multiple buckets (One bucket for each tech).
He/She has to pick a bucket to solve all the
Questions within it / as many quesions as
he/she can...!!!” – Close!! So close!!
Keep guessing and come up with more
thoughts and ideas in this forum thread. Who
knows? You might win a goodie!

TExeter Blogging Contest
Finally, the blogs are here. TExeter proudly
presents you ESI Technical Blogs. Share all the
interesting innovation you do on a daily basis
and let your peers avoid reinventing the wheels.
Of course, the pride that is going to come along
is a clear add on. Here are the rules:

Write your blog in a plain word document,
and email the blog word doc to the TExeter
team. Post a response on this thread once
you have emailed your blog document to
TExeter team.

Keep it FAT (Fun Authentic Tech), and
keep it short :) - No more than 300 words
please!

Feel free to add pictures, gifs and code
snippets within the blog doc



Keep it Fun. Authentic.Tech.
