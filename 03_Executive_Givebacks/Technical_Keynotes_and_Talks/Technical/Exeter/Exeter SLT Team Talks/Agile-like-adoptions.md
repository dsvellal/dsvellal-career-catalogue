## Slide 17: Defining the process

- A process is essentially an integrated set of roles, methods and techniques to impart into a process
- This helps us achieve the following:
- Minimize risk
- Accurately estimate project schedule (and budget)
- Detect problems early
- Transparent and clear communication
- Track progress and detect slippage early
- Effectively and efficiently achieve project goals
