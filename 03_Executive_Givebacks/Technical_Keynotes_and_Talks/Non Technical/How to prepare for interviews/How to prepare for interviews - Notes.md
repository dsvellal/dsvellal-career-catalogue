# How to prepare for interviews - Notes

> Converted from `How to prepare for interviews - Notes.docx`


Before the interview


Update your resume


Keep it results driven, infographics helps, more than one flavor of your resume based on company, highlight what you have done, print your resume & get it reviewed by peers & friends.


Create a preparation list


Common data-structures, algorithms & why/how they are used, understand different libraries of the language of your choice, be familiar with more than one language, practice designing simple applications.


Practice online coding & attend online competitions.


Talk to engineers & managers to understand the current trend.


Practice coding on paper, keep your online profiles updated.


Prepare for common HR questions. Be genuine & honest. Project positivity, confidence and willingness to learn in your answers.


Research your company, what profile they are offering & research your interviewer as well.


During the interview


Project positivity.


Be confident & honest.


Ask questions, don’t assume anything!


Look for clues the interviewer gives!


Articulate your approach to solving the problem.


Understand the intent of the question & address it!


Always try to solve the problem & then optimize!


If you genuinely believe what you say, politely push back!


Think architecture, think customer first, think abstraction!


There is always a trade-off and there is NEVER an IDEAL solution!


Don’t give up!


After the interview


Politely ask when you can hear back.


Request for feedback on points of improvements if any.


Identify what questions you were not able to answer, find answers to them.


Identify additional topics for preparation & work on it.


Brush up & prepare for your next interview!


In interviews, not getting selected is not just because you are not good, it’s also because of an opinion someone has, after a brief interaction with you. It’s OK! Learn from the mistakes, correct them & don’t repeat them again.
