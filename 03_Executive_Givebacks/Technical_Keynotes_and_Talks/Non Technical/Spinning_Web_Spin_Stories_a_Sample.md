Long ago, but not so long ago, there lived a farmer Siddegowdaraaya Chennabasavappa howLi,
who was world famous in Gulburga! He had raised a few chickens, a few cows and a cat. Our
story is about this EVIL NINJA CAT – Cat-astrophe!. The hens believed in the saying “Early bird
gets the worm” so without disturbing Siddegowdaraaya Chennabasavappa howLi, one morning
they quietly unlocked the doors of their shelter and started pecking for worms (picture 1 showing
the hens pecking gracefully)

Our Evil Ninja Cat, seeing his mates up so early in the morning thought, “Hmm.. why not try a
radical experiment ??”. He decided to cross-breed humans and hens. Now, he already had the
DNA of the hens, but how would he get the DNA of a human being ?? It was tricky, but he had a
few tricks up his sleeves, erm.. or paws! Using his Ninja techniques, he finally managed to get a
stand of his master’s hair (picture 2 showing the ninja techniques)

Cat-astrophe then meditated to recall the forbidden technique of DNA merger. This meditation
gave him super-cat strength to use the cursed technique. With all the raw ingredients ready, he
chanted the tabooed mantra, and placed his “SEAL” on the two objects containing the DNA.
(picture 3 showing the SEAL placed by cat-astrophe on the two DNA objects)
