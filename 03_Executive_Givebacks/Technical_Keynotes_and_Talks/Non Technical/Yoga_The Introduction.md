The essence of ashTAnga Yoga!

Sage Patanjali's ashTAnga yoga
UNION
SAMADHI : Thoughtless state
DHYANA: Meditation
DHARANA: Inward concentration
PRATYAHARA: Withdrawal of sense organs
PRANAYAMA: Breath Control
ASANA: Physical Posture / bodily control
NIYAMA: Cleanliness, Contentment, Self-Study, Austerity, Surrenderance
YAMA: Truth, Non-Violence, Non-Covetousness, Non-Possessiveness, Celibacy

May all be free from sickness.
