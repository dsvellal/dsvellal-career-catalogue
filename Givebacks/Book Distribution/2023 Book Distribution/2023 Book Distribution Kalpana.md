Sir,
Please find herewith enclosed a Receipt bearing No.1242, dated
19-04-2023 for a sum of Rs.2,000/- being the remittance made by you
through RTGS to our Trust. Also find herewith enclosed a copy of the
communication dated 14-07-2010 issued by the Commissioner of
Income-Tax, Mysuru, exempting your contribution to our Trust under
Section 80G of the Income-Tax Act, for your information and record.
The amount contributed by you will be appropriated for
distribution of note books and reading materials for children studying
in Government Schools of Rural Areas during this year. We shall keep
you informed about the programme for distribution of note books and
reading materials, which is to take place for the whole of the month
June 2023 and you too can participate in the same to have a firsthand
impression about our activities.
Our Trust is highly thankful to you for this benevolent gesture
and we hope that you will extend similar co-operation in future also.
