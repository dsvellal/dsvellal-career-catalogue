Introduction
•

This report is a record of Dattatreya's development assessment from the Leadership
Development center. The report gauges Dattatreya's leadership capability against the Philips
leadership and culture behaviors.

•

The comments in this report regarding Dattatreya's competence at the different
assessments and exercises reflect the professional judgements and interpretations of the
lead assessor and lead observer. They are based on performance during the development
center activities. The individual has also completed the Swift Management Aptitude,
Decision Styles and Strengths Insights online questionnaires. Whilst these methods give
greater and more accurate insight than can normally be obtained at an interview alone, they
are not infallible. A digital copy of each of these reports has also been shared with you. If
you have any detailed questions specifically about one of these reports, please reach out to
Agusty Rebekah (E: agusty.rebekah@philips.com).

•

This profile has been designed to provide a rounded picture of this individual. It seeks to
identify their aspirations and operating style, as well as highlighting areas for development.
This profile should be interpreted in conjunction with other sources of information and
should be regarded as valid for up to two years.

Executive summary
A customer focused and data driven individual, throughout the development center you
demonstrated your desire to add value for the customers of Philips. Your data orientation enables
you to take a well-informed and confident approach to the challenges and opportunities you face.
This is further supported by your highly accountable approach, which sees you proactively
identifying areas where you can add greater value by challenging the status quo. You demonstrate a
genuine desire to take personal responsibility and ownership for driving improvement and positive
change. Furthermore, your significant strength in the area of being articulate enables you to
effectively break down complexity for others, and to create a safe and open space to have
conversations. That being said, this is a strength of which you should be more aware of the risk of
overusing, as actually your approach to more senior stakeholders can trigger surprising responses to
you, and responses which are not going to enable you to drive the change which is required. You
could benefit from further leveraging your reflective approach in order to implement the insights
you gather about yourself and others to influence the way you interact with different stakeholders.
Furthermore, your ability to break down complexity into simple steps can at times be a barrier to
you connecting with others on the bigger picture, and therefore the purpose of the change you are
seeking to drive, as others can get caught up on the 'how'.
In order to further develop your impact across Philips, you could benefit from supporting others to
recognize the 'why' you are driving specific change. Oftentimes you have the numbers in your own
head which you have used to draw valid conclusions, however others may not have sight of these
and therefore do not understand the motivation. In addition, you may be at risk of overusing your
focus on taking responsibility, which can result in you taking ownership for things you could
empower others to do. Seeking to better understand others' expectations and perceptions, as well
as being more willing to have challenging conversations, could support you in this area.
