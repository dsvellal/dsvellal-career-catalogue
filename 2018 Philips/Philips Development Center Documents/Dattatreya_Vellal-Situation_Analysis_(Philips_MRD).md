Your Responses
1.What is the current situation within the Company? It may be useful to identify the Strengths; Weaknesses;
Opportunities and Threats (SWOT) to address this question.
Strength: Proclas has a strong user base across 25 countries worldwide and further 20 more countries via partnerships with
domestic networks. They have invested in acquiring companies and have built on strong infrastructure, and have invested to
acquire universal mobile telephone service licences awarded by the governments in Europe. Leadership in these areas have
allowed them to offer, to their customers, a wide range of products and a completely customizable deals, which their current
competitors do not have. Their ﬁnancials are increasing year-on-year, where, in the last-year, their contributions to ﬁnances
from Rest of the world has been very signiﬁcant, showing the strength of expansion outside EU. The company is showing
positive trends of resource development & learning opportunities, as indicated by the engagement survey conducted in
Belgium.
Weakness: The organization has difﬁculty in delivering securities within the margin that's required. They have very stringent
SLAs for response to customers, however, the supporting pieces of software and systems are outdated and aren't matching
up to the needs of the hour. Customer focus has decreased in the last year, by 7% in the Europe division, which is their primary
area of strength.
Opportunities: Clearly, optimizing the operational costs is an untapped opportunity. With the capabilities of AI, there are
possibilities of addressing some identiﬁed weaknesses, and convert them into opportunities of providing world class
operational support to the customer base worldwide. Also, there are opportunities of entering into untapped markets,
currently, the revenue models from Rest of the world, is the highest for the year 20x+0, and a clear indication that there's a
huge opportunity of expansion to other countries.
Threat: Outdated infrastructures in the operations is becoming a very quick challenge the company has to address and can
quickly become a threat as the company may not be able to uphold the trust of the customers. Ironing out some software
maintenance related issues are also becoming very important, as evidenced by the root-cause of the noted 24 hours outage.
Convincing long standing customers to stay with the company is also going be a challenge since there are rumours of
organisations thinking of ending contracts with Proclas, and managing the media and messaging during the time of crisis.
Also, the company is losing its customer base by not reacting fast to the situation. This can be capitalized by the competitors
and can have an impact on the user-base. Other competitors are quickly catching up with their disruptive offerings, and
indulgence in AI for offering better support.

3.What should the actions and priorities be for 1) the next three months and 2) for the next two years? Why
are these priorities?
Next three months:
- Set up a process in place, to ensure that any similar situation can be handled properly with the external world, and
internally, is root-caused correctly and the core concerns are addressed so that they do not repeat.
- Have a conversation with ARE JLCR group to identify what their pain points are, and how we can work towards improving the
service.
- Win back trust of existing customers and ensure that our existing customer base doesn't diminish.
- Understand customer pain points identiﬁed by queries from customers through different channels and try to address the
low-hanging fruits to maintain and build on customer trust.
- Look for opportunities to help customer service representatives to easily ﬁnd data, so that their SLAs are met and the
customer expectations are better managed.
Next two years:
- Invest in upgrading outdated infrastructure.
- Invest in identifying strategies of optimizing operational costs through: a) Strategic AI interventions, b) making information
more available and usable, c) self-service mode of troubleshooting & correction.
- Strategize to upgrade capacity of IP transport network to start supporting 5G spectrum and aggressively promote IoT, in
parallel work with the ofﬁce for housing quality of life, environment, and energy to understand the reason behind the fear,
and partner with them to help facilitate the auction of 5G infrastructure.
- Work towards acquisition of maverick telecom service providers and integrate their strengths into Proclas.
- Strategize how we can promote ﬁxed lines, data usage, mobile phone contract sales - as these are long standing an long
engagement revenue models.
- Identify opportunities of expansion in India, and other under-tapped markets in the rest of the world.
- Strategize on reducing direct costs & overheads to ensure that our operating proﬁts increase.
- Identify why the operating cost model in UK & Ireland is working out for us, and replicate it in the rest of the regions.

4.What would be your plan to implement the strategy? What are your short and long term goals?
As the vice president of Proclas Telecommunications, Belgium, the following are my short term and long term goals:
Short term:
- Improve customer service representative response times by upgrading infrastructure
- Work with the IT team in connecting live chats answers to websites' live chat function, social media queries to customer
cases, ensure that different responses through multiple routes are connected to a customer case.
- Understand why customer response time increased by 7%, and take necessary actions to bring this down.
- Deﬁne clear leadership & future directions through different connects with employees and leadership teams
- Promote customer focus within the employees through different programs and improve the engagement scores on
customer focus.
- Work with the HR department to roll out programs to understand why there's a reduction in effectiveness of management &
team cohesion within departments and address them through multiple programs.
- Lobby with regulators in Belgium to ensure that fewer, larger carriers are essential for a healthy telecoms market.
Long term:
- Increase the no. of subscribers in Belgium by 10%, through aggressive data and mobile contracts, and attractive mobile
handset offerings.
- Take our learnings from connecting responses from different routes to a customer case, and see if we can achieve
something similar in other regions as well.
- Improve our IP transport network capacity to support 5G spectrum, and start rolling out offers, plans and upgrades, as soon
as its made available.
- Partner with the ofﬁce of housing quality of life, environment and energy to understand the fears, and how Procals can help
in addressing the fears related to 5G spectrum.
- Conduct independent studies, and partner with consumers, health specialists, politicians to help reduce the fears of 5G
networks on the health of the citizens and environment.

5.What will you do to ensure this plan is successful?
* Setup a team to upgrade outdated infrastructure of our customer service representatives, with a clear goal to help them
work with the systems better.
* Setup a team to look at opportunities of connecting data from multiple sources and tie it back to our customer-case.
* Setup an analytics team, to understand key common problems that are prevalent from our customer-cases, and see how we
can proactively address them.
* Setup a research wing to understand how 5G spectrum affects health, and partner with independent research organizations
to help conduct studies that'll beneﬁt our company.
* Increase funding to the network infrastructure team, and provide clear goals of upgrading the network capacity to support
5G spectrum.
* Setup a research wing to identify how we can start adopting AI models and reduce our operational costs.
* Setup a chain of command and control to ensure that all actions are being followed through & results are reaching our
customers, evidenced by our increased customer engagements and reduced operational costs.
* Fund marketing team and provide directions to help increase the subscriber base in Belgium
* Partner with other VPs and socialize some ideas of improvements being taken in Belgium and how we can repeat the same
in the region of their inﬂuence.
* Fund political parties and other inﬂuential organizations to promote our cause of larger carriers for essential healthy
telecoms market.
* Work with HR team to roll out programs to improve team cohesion within departments & promote effective management.
