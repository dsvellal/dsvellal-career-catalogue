Dear Sutra Extension team,

Thank you for your interest in the XITE program and the timely submission of your application. We have completed the Research screen which included input from one or more key Business stakeholders. Based on this review and careful consideration, we will not advance this proposal further.

The rationale for this decision is as follows: While this idea was viewed as impactful for AV&I, it was ranked lower in priority vs other submissions for the category. Additionally, there were questions about why this solution was being developed in-house as opposed to leveraging the different external tools being used to drive internal operation efficiencies in EI which is the standard approach in EI.

We value your participation and hope you will consider applying again in the future. To support any future application, we’ve provided feedback on sections of the application that could be further strengthened.

Application section
Comments
Customer Need
This section can be improved by including quantitative metrics to support the need for this solution. For example, quantify the inefficiecies e.g how long is the current process? How many steps? how many FTE required?
Customer
Improve this slide by specifying the BU teams that would use the solution.
Market opportunity
This slide should capture and quantify the potential benefits of the solution - what steps in the process are improved? What metrics will improve?
Why Philips
 While the proposal mentions explicit requests from AV&I, it does not articulate why this solution should be developed in house business consequences for Philips if this gap remains unaddressed. Also it is more like internal operational imporvement,  not directly customer-facing innovation.

Thank you for your hard work throughout this process and we hope to work with you on a future XITE project.
