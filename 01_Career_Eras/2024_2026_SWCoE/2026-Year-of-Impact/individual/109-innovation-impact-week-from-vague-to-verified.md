### Session Description
> **What can you expect:** Creating high-quality requirement specifications is complex and time-consuming. Teams must balance business intent, user needs, regulatory requirements and technical feasibility—often resulting in unclear requirements, missed gaps and long review cycles. In this session, you will discover how the Innovation Engineering team uses Kairos to support smarter requirement drafting with AI. Using a predefined prompt for IEC 62304 and FDA compliance, teams can structure, review and strengthen their requirements more effectively.
>
> **Who should watch the recording:** Colleagues involved in defining, reviewing or managing requirements. Especially relevant for **product owners, system and software architects, V&V teams, Quality & Regulatory professionals**, and engineers working with IEC 62304, FDA requirements and software compliance processes.
