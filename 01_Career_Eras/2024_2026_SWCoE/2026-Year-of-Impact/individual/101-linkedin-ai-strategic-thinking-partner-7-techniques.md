### 7 Techniques Presented

#### 1. Devil's Advocate
Explicitly instruct AI to identify flaws in proposals. Use AI to stress-test your own ideas before presenting them.

#### 2. Pre-Mortem
Ask AI to explain hypothetical project failures. "Imagine this project failed — what went wrong?"

#### 3. Stakeholder Simulation
Have AI roleplay skeptical decision-makers. Practice your pitch against simulated pushback.

#### 4. Assumption Surfacing
Leverage AI's external perspective to question taken-for-granted beliefs. Expose hidden assumptions.

#### 5. Scenario Modeling
Test plans against realistic disruptions. "What if budget is cut 30%? What if key person leaves?"

#### 6. Second Opinion
Force AI to argue opposing viewpoints. Get the counter-argument before your stakeholders do.

#### 7. Confidence Calibration
Rate AI's certainty levels for verification needs. Know when to trust and when to verify.
