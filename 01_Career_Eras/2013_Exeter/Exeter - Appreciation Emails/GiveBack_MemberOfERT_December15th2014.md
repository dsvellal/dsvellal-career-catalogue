Dear all,
Here’s some of the points that came out as a part of today’s discussion.
As an ERT team, we should:
1. Define situations / categories under which we react.
a. Right now, I can think of the following categories:
i. Fire emergencies – Includes building/floor on fire, mock-drills etc.
ii. Medical emergencies – Someone injures themselves, person fainting, or any other medical
emergencies
iii. Situational emergencies – Any situation related accidents etc.
2. Identify and come up with process for each of them
3. Identify and define protocols (steps) that we have to take in each of these situations
4. Meet more often (Once a month – and evaluate where we are, and what we should do next)
5. Arrange and get certified to conduct medical procedures like CPR, pulse reading, heart rate monitoring etc.
6. Get familiarized with company policies like – availing sick-room-facility, whom to co-ordinate with etc.
7. Build an information base which consists of vital employee information that all of ERT members can leverage
in case of an incident – this consists of emergency contact numbers, insurance policy details, blood group,
where does the person stay etc.
8. Acknowledge the participation of every ERT member on a yearly or 6 monthly basis
9. Create a rotation plan for members to move out, and induct new members into the system
