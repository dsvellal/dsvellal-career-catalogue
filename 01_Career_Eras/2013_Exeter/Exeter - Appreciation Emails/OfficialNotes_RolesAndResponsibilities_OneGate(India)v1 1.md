2 Roles and Responsibilities of key members from the
leadership team
2.1

Anuroop Gaonkar
His role will be that of a PMO whose responsibilities will comprise of and not necessarily be limited to:
a) Planning and Execution of regular releases – He will be accountable for all updates and outcomes
from the regular release cycles of OneGate. Regular release cycles do not include hotfixes and
patches. He is also responsible for approving any new processes being implemented.
b) Resource Management – He will be accountable for allocation and tracking of all resources. Any
other function requiring resources will have to reach out to him for allocation before utilizing the
resources.
c) Quality Assurance – He will be accountable for the Quality assurance function at OneGate and will
be accountable for the quality of output. His responsibilities shall cover all areas of functional and
non-functional testing for the product as may be necessary as per prioirities on the ground. Is also
responsible for any process change that happens on the ground.
d) Technical/Functional Initiatives – He will be sharing these initiatives between himself and Krishna.
For any one initiative, there will be one point of accountability.
e) Release Management and Configuration Management – He will be accountable for this function for
all the major releases and responsible for ensuring that configuration is managed taking into account
hotfixes and patches.
In addition to these, he will be required to play a key role in the evolution of the high performance team
which may go beyond the responsibilities discussed above. Any process changes that are approved by him
need an express approval by the Core team.

2.2

Krishnamurthy Hegde
His role will be that of a Solutions Architect whose responsibilities will comprise of and not necessarily
be limited to:
a) Support regular releases – He will be accountable to ensure that all the functional specifications
necessary for the release are reviewed and finalized on time. Any escalations here will go to Anuroop
and the EG PMO.
b) Product Support – He is accountable for meeting the SLA on the support tickets along with his team.
He is accountable for providing JIRA/RT priorities to the PMO for execution and will the primary
point of contact to liaise with the stakeholder on EG side.
c) Hotfix and patch development – Post assignment of resources by the PMO for this activity, he is
accountable for the delivery of the hotfix and the quality thereof. He is also accountable for ensuring
proper configuration management while merging with the mainstream releases.

3 Roles and Responsibilities of Core Team Members
The OneGare core team is a group of people who are/will be instrumental in providing momentum to the
initiatives in OneGate. This is an aspirational group, members of which may end up having formal or informal
roles for making things better in OneGate. Such roles may be individual or team based. The constitution of this
team is the prerogative of the head of product development based on needs and priorities on the ground. The size
of this shall be limited to a few and we will look at shuffling people in and out of this group based on needs,
priorities and capabilities that we have in our current team.Most of the initiatives driven in the team will be
initiated, guided , reviewed and approved in the core team irrespective of the source of the idea.

3.1

Amit Sharma
Amit Sharma is the Siebel stack owner and accountable for all the work execution that happens in the
Siebel team.
His responsibilities will comprise of and not necessarily be limited to:
a) Allocation and delivery (design, development and unit testing) of work from the Siebel team in

4 Roles and Responsibilities of the Support Team
4.1

Support Lead
The selected individual’s responsibility shall include but not be restricted to the following areas:
• Managing delivery SLA for the support team
• Provide in depth support to Exeter clients and employees in troubleshooting and resolving issues
• Document issues and solutions within the tracking system and follow issue progress through
resolution
• Develop effective working relationships with other Exeter staff, onshore and offshore
• Work closely with other team members to deliver outstanding support for clients
• Work both independently as needed and as an active, contributing member of a team.
• Actively contribute and work to continually improve customer self-help through the publication of
technically and functionally accurate knowledge base articles
• Supporting QC activities for the product when ticket volumes are low
• Manage transition of defects and enhancements to the product engineering team and facilitate
delivery of fixes

4.2

Support Analyst
The selected individual’s responsibility shall include but not be restricted to the following areas:
• Meeting the SLA committed to customers
