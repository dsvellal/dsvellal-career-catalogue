Hello Prasad, George, RNS, Shekhar,
We completed the 3 day session on Managing stress better by adopting yoga practices. I have attached the feedback
of the participants.
To me, personally, it was a very satisfying session and I got an opportunity to share whatever little I knew. I would
like to thank the management for considering the idea and supporting it through its completion. The feedback from

help in addressing stress better, and the session clearly has achieved the intent. Overall it was a good session and
folks have expressed interest in future sessions like these.

to tackle stress better.

above. The agenda and other details of the workshop are listed below. For getting a fair idea on the logistics, please
vote "Yes" or "No" to the voting option created with this mail also optionally respond back to me (only) via mail
