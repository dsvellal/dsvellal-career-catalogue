Subramanya Vellal, Dattatreya
Software Excellence Competency Lead

In Philips, with our focus changing from healthcare to health-tech, Talent Acquisition,
in association with the Software Center of Excellence, embarked on a journey in
2020 and have piloted the first Philips Bar Raiser Program with a 10-member team in
Philips Innovation Campus in Bangalore. This was part of adopting best practices in
the technology space and strengthening our focus on the "Workforce of the Future".
After initiation, TA has successfully replicated the program across the US, EMEA and
Blumenau.
Note: This program, it's initiation & journey so far was featured in an article:
"Become a Bar Raiser - The Quintessential Talent Ambassador" published in
the Global CTO News, September 2020.
Also, read about what's in it for the bar-raisers, via the voice-of-bar-raisers page

On this page, we have documented endorsements that the bar-raiser program
has received from leaders, the candidates who have attended the bar-raiser
interview, as well as the hiring managers who have been part of the bar-raiser
interview loop.
