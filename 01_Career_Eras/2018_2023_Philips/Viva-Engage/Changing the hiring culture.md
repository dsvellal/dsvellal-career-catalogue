The journey started with asking ourselves the following questions: How to get the best software talent? and How
do we provide the best experience for them?
What has evolved is a technique that is helping change the hiring culture at Philips. We are moving away from the
traditional techniques, to a more engaged conversation, data-driven feedbacks, quantitative analysis of the
discussions and consensus driven hiring decisions. This article talks about changes happening in the hiring
culture.
Read about why the clock is ticking and how leaders, hiring managers and candidates are talking about the
program in the Endorsements of bar raiser page. Also, the program, it's initiation & journey so far was featured in
an article: "Become a Bar Raiser - The Quintessential Talent Ambassador" published in the Global CTO News,
September 2020.

On this page, we take you through the process we have adopted to improve our hiring culture at Philips.

A note about de-brief
A de-brief is a meeting where, the entire interview panelist, along with the bar-raiser and the hiring manager joins
and collaborates over the hire/no-hire decision based on data. The following sections talk about the structure of
the de-brief, conflict resolutions during de-briefs and highlights some lessons learnt.

Suggested approach
First 5 minutes, Exchange interview notes: TA provides the Bar Raiser and panelists with individual interview
assessment copies, each having a clear hire/no hire decision about the candidate.

Next 10 minutes, Discussion & Voting: Each panelist states her/his decision and votes ‘for’ OR ‘against’ the
Hire and explains the rationale behind the decision
Final 10 minutes, Decision stage: Bar Raiser and Hiring manager take into account the inputs from previous
panelists and communicate the hire/no-hire decision to TA/HR
Closing the loop with the candidate: The TA/HR then takes the decision and closes the loop with the
candidate, conveying the hire/no-hire decisions, and any other constructive feedback that the panel has
decided to be passed on to the candidate.

Conflict resolution
In case the debrief results in conflicting views between the Hiring Manager and the Bar Raiser, the following steps
are suggested:
HR/TA representative organizes a ‘’deep dive” which involves HM, Bar Raiser and HR Manager.
The risk of hiring/not hiring is clearly articulated and documented by the Bar Raiser to the panel.
In exceptional cases, if the Hiring Manager is still convinced about his/her decision and wants to move
ahead, the risk is documented and captured on Avature by TA/HR for future review and analysis.

Some lessons learnt
Bar Raisers need to have the ability to drive consensus through critical reasoning
The Talent acquisition representative and Bar Raiser shall alternately play the role of “content curators”
when discussions move off tangent
The debrief is best conducted within 2-3 days of the final interview
Bar Raisers need to meet at regular intervals to refine and perfect the structure of these sessions and the
format of feedback inputs.
