Resourcefulness, being a "Role Model" to others, having an
appetite to take calculated risk and a "Not afraid to fail" attitude.
Similarly, a prized organization is one, which can attract and
retain such employees. The U.S. Department of Labor estimates
that a bad hire can cost an organization up to 30% of the
employee's potential ﬁrst-year expected earnings. Hence, it is
very critical to attract the right talent.
Amazon started its Bar Keeper program in 1999 by creating a
group of interviewers, who could identify the right set of
candidates that ﬁt the organization's culture and leadership
parameters. Because the purpose of the program was to raise the
standard with every new hire and not maintain it, the Bar Keepers
eventually became the Bar Raisers. This meant that candidates

In Philips, with our focus changing from healthcare to healthtech,
Talent Acquisition, in association with the Software Center of
Excellence, embarked on a similar journey in 2020. They have
piloted the ﬁrst Philips Bar Raiser Program with a 10-member
team in Philips Innovation Campus in Bangalore. This was part of
adopting best practices in the technology space and strengthening
our focus on the "Workforce of the Future". After initiation, TA
has successfully replicated the program across the US, EMEA and
Blumenau.

Milestone One: Initiation: One of the most critical and timeintensive phases was identifying the Bar Raisers. These individuals

behaviors, which constantly takes us closer to our mission.
