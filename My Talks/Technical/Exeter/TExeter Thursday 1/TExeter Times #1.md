Birth & vision of TExeter!
A few months ago, in a meeting room, we were sitting together and pondering over an
interesting problem statement. This is how the conversation went.
George – “We are a technology company with a diverse technical experience. How can we
nurture and sustain this so that technology becomes an infection within us and the expertise
grows!”
One of us – “How about we build a forum to exchange ideas!”
Another – “We can also conduct an idea Hack-a-thon, where people do focused development
to address real-life technical problems that ESI faces day-day”

Thursday June 26th – A grand beginning! The First
TExeter Thursday event! More details on the success
story, testimonials and photos on Page 3.

TExeter Forum is up!

Another – “We can also have a poster presentation contest….”
Another – “We have to make sure that we establish a channel through which anyone can
publish helps / requests / problems / solutions / guidelines / best practices which they have
encountered”
Another – “We also have to establish a platform to support cross project collaboration! A place
where people can reach out, others can answer, interact and socialize anything that will help
build the technical vibe!”
Another – “How about we use this forum to also identify and recognize people who have
contributed to the technical growth? Can we use this to drive technical talks and other
technical activities?”
George – “Let’s give this a structure and then roll it out”
So you see, in this conversation, we have tried to give you the essence of TExeter and what led
to its birth. We have also outlined the vision that TExeter wishes to achieve. The TExeter team
has many events/activities in its pipeline to achieve these. Keep your eyes and mind open
because we will surprise you! Soon!

the long term TExeter objective – “Cultivate and share
technology practices across projects in a less formal, fun
filled environment”.

Quiz it forward….

To keep the spirit of TExeter we are opening a Quiz me - running
contest... The rules are this:
- You ask a question
- The person who replies with the answer asks another question
- The chain continues
TExeter will monitor this queue to make sure that the person who
asks maximum questions and answers max answer gets recognized
and perhaps given a goodie as well!
So, why wait... Quiz along!
