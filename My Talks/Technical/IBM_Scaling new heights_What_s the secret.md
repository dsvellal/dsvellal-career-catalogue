ATTITUDE
1. Be enthusiastic
-

Whatever you do, do it with
all your strength
Speak up when you have a
contribution
Say it like you mean it!
Be early to meetings
Sit in the front row
Smile ☺
16

ATTITUDE
2. Be positive
– Think about things that build up
your project, not tear it down
– Withhold negative comments
– Use constructive criticism – don’t
bring up a problem without a
potential solution
– Compliment people when they do a
good job

17

ATTITUDE
3. Be nice
– It is easier to be nice than to be mean
– People are more likely to co-operate with
you if you are nice
– People are more likely to tell you the truth
about problems if you don’t yell at them
– Say things in a way that does not make
people feel bad

18

ATTITUDE
4. Be internally motivated
– Do all things well
– Things may seem bad at times, but
you must still continue to make
progress
– Paint a picture of how nice things will
be when the task is all done!!
– If a task is necessary but boring,
make a game of doing it very well

19

ATTITUDE
5. Be Honest and Fair
– Your reputation is made one
interaction at a time
– Act like IBM is your business
– Once people know you as unfair or
dishonest, it is very difficult to recover

20

ATTITUDE
6. Never give up
– Keep on trying, even if
the goal looks hard
– Never give up, never
surrender
– Do it in small pieces, not
all at once
– Perseverance
– Try an alternative plan
