## Slide 7: A few career myths!

- Something is wrong with me if I don’t know what I want yet.
- There is only ONE career for me.
- If I choose a career, I’ll be stuck with it forever.
- I’ve failed if I make a wrong choice.
- My first career will be right for my whole life.
- There is an expert who can tell me what to do with the rest of my life.

## Slide 13: Assessments

- Interests – Do you prefer to
- Work in a hands-on environment with objects, machines, tools, plants, or animals or to be outdoors – THE DOER!
- Observe, learn, investigate, analyze, evaluate, or solve problems – THE THINKER!
- Work with information or data, organizing and carrying things out in detail to facilitate decision making – THE ORGANIZER!
- 13


## Slide 14: Assessments

- Interests – Do you prefer to
- Work in unstructured situations using your imagination or creativity, be artistic, innovative – THE CREATOR!
- Work with people influencing, persuading or performing or leading or managing for organizational goals or for economic gain – THE PERSUADER!
- Work with people – to inform, enlighten, help, train, develop or cure them, or are skilled with words – THE HELPER!
