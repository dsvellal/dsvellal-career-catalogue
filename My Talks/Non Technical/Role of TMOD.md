Role of TMOD
YOU ARE THE EMCEE !!

Preparation
Before the meeting
At the meeting
During the meeting
After the meeting

Before the meeting
Agenda
Special themes
Contact (and confirm)
Speakers
Evaluators
General Evaluator
Timer
Grammarian
Ah counter

At the meeting
Arrive early
Confirm speakers and their project title
Incorporate last minute change

Take your place as TMOD and be ready ☺

During the meeting
Take control !
Maintain the energy levels and mood
Make your audience feel that things are going well
Lead the applause after an announcement
Remain standing at the presentation area
Acknowledge anyone who comes / leaves the presentation area
Address folks with names – TM, CTM, CL, AL etc.
Do not use – MTM / Mr / Ms. Etc..
Avoid using
None other than..
Lets put our hands together..
Can we give a round of applause..

Ad-lib after each section (meta-speaker)
Introduce the TTM – take control back after the section ends
Conclude and hand the control over to the president

After the meeting
Stay and socialize for a few minutes
Update the winners list on our communities /
facebook
Guide the next Toastmaster of the day:
Your preparations
What went well
What can be improved

Remember
The 30 seconds audience time for Speaker feedback
To ask the timer for the report after each section
To ask the audience to vote after each section
To Occupy the Lectern always!
To Acknowledge the role-players and speakers
The hand-shake!!
