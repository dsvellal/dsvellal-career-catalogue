The relaxation response
You can’t avoid all stress, but you can
counteract its negative effects by learning
how to evoke the relaxation response, a
state of deep rest that is the polar opposite
of the stress response.

Stress Management & Yoga
Mindfulness meditation (Dhyana)
Mindfulness is the quality of being fully
engaged in the present moment, without
analyzing or otherwise “over-thinking” the
experience
Meditation that cultivates mindfulness is
particularly effective at reducing stress,
anxiety, depression, and other negative
emotions.

Stress Management & Yoga
Guided imagery (Special form of Dhyana)
Visualization, or guided imagery, is a
variation on traditional meditation that can
help relieve stress.
When used as a relaxation technique,
guided imagery involves imagining a
scene in which you feel at peace, free to
let go of all tension and anxiety.
