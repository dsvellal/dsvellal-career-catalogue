Factors that helped me land a Tier 1 Job

Resume – create your persona!

Let’s play a game!
Imagine you are the interviewer.
You have to interview a person
in the next 10 minutes. What
characteristics can you identify
in each of the following
resumes?

Resume characteristics
• Resume is a conversation starter
– Flipkart manager broke ice via his interest in yoga!

• Keep it short & result driven
– Resume readers don’t have the patience

• Get creative!
– Not beyond two pages
– Info graphics work like a charm
– Hyperlink where-ever necessary
– Prepare different flavors of your resume
– Highlight things that you have done different, from others

• Review your resume, get it reviewed – it helps!
• Print your resume! See how it looks!

Prepare a preparation list
• Can you answer these?
– Largest no. in a stream of nos?
• Use max-heap (O(nlogn)) – know your data structures!

– All paths of a maze?
• Use BFS algorithm – know your algorithms well!

– Generic Arrays sorting?
• Use java.util.Arrays – know one language well!

– JSP, HTML – how are they connected?
• Develop familiarity with other languages

– Design google maps?
• Prepare for design questions

– What scenarios would you consider before designing a
bookmyshow like app?
• Prepare for scenario based discussion

Practice online, attend competitions

•

Customizable difficulty levels
Develop familiarity to data-structures

• Regular challenges and follow ups
• Compare results with others
• View other solutions & learn

Talk to engineers & managers!
• To understand:
– The current technology trends
– Candidate competencies industry is interested in

• To build your network
– They have contacts to HRs & Managers
– They know folks from other companies!
When you reach this position, give back and help others!

Practice Paper Coding
• Build familiarity with popular APIs
• Brings clarity of thought
• Builds accuracy & speed
• Write clean code

Keep your online profile updated

They create opportunity for you!

Practice common interview questions
• Some common questions include
– Tell me about yourself
– Why are you the right candidate for this job
– What projects have you worked on
– What are your weaknesses
– What are your strengths
– Tell me about a time when
• You failed
• You had a conflict with your superior

• In your answers, project:
– Positivity
– Willingness to learn & adapt
– Be honest & genuine

Research your company & the interviewer

During the interview
•
•
•
•
•
•
•
•
•
•

Project positivity
Be confident & honest
Ask questions, don’t assume anything!
Look for clues the interviewer gives!
Articulate your approach to solving the problem
Understand the intent of the question & address it!
Always try to solve the problem & then optimize!
If you genuinely believe what you say, politely push back!
Think architecture, think customer first, think abstraction!
There is always a trade-off and there is NEVER an IDEAL solution!

Don’t give up!

After the interview
• Politely ask when you can hear back
• Ask the HR/Interviewer to share interview feedback
• Learn answers to questions you failed to address
• Add additional topics to your practice list
• Brush yourself up and prepare for the next one!

What to do when you get a reject!

There are other
awesome companies
where you can learn a
lot. Give your best!

Finally!
• Identify what role you want to play. Now and 5
years from now!
• Have a plan & define priorities! It’s important!
– Want to be in US in next 5 years and earn in $
– Want to travel the world
– Want to settle down in India & focus on something
else, while I get a constant income from job
– Want to spend time with family

• Always work towards what you want –don’t lose
focus!
• Help others – knowledge spreads by sharing!
