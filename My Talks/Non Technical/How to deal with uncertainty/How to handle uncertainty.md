## Slide 5: 9 point plan: How to deal with uncertainty!

- Reflect on past success
- Snapshot your successes
- Focus on what matters, focus on the present
- It’s ok not to be perfect!
- Think about solutions instead of dwelling on problems!
- Meditate (on anything!)
- Develop new skills
- Be kind to yourself
- Engage in healing/self-care
- Accept uncertainty – embrace it – move on!
