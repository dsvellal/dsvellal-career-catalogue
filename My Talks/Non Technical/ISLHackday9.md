What is HackDay?

 HackDay is a mindset.
– What’s a Hack: Anything under the sun!

 It's when you have an idea and you want to do something about it.

 We plan a day, this time on October 7th, 2011, November 11th 2011

 It's your time to spend to turn idle thoughts and wishes into reality.

Is it just for a single day ?? (As Robi says - Ah! a trick question!)
 The answer is yes and no.
 A target to shoot for – October 7th 2011, November 11th 2011
 Start whenever you have time!
 Complete them before Hackday!!
– Do it before October 7th 2011 – for global hackday evaluations
– Do it before November 11th 2011 – for ISL local hackday evaluations
 Voting phase at global hackday
– IBMers vote for your hacks. Hacks with max votes wins!
 Continue even after hackday – the base is set!

Who can participate ?
Anyone… Everyone.
Coder… designer
Thinker… tinkerer
Builder… evaluator
A student… teacher
We'll even take managers who want to dust of their rusty skills!
Executives looking to jump into the fray with others!

HackDay is open for all (well, anyone employed by IBM for now). If you
have the time and motivation, you can participate in HackDay. No bar on
age or experience!!

Erm.. Wait.. I don’t know how to program!!
 That’s ok! Neither do I ☺
 Despite the name, HackDay isn't just limited to those with technical skills.
 Anything can become a HackDay project.
– You can design a new logo for a product,
– Offer a new layout for a web page,
– A new and more efficient process for something old and outdated.
– You can create a presentation that teaches people something new
– Write a blog post about how to use an existing system efficiently.
– Record a video on how you make your office environment efficient
– Give step by step instructions for how you built your standing desk (and
how it's worked for you so far).

Ok! I am ready.. Where do I start ??
 Start now by :
– Registering a project
– Checking out other projects, see if you want to join any ?
– Registering your team, a few may be looking for more members to join to tackle a
project.
– Checking out other Local Events
– Checking out Hackday Contests
 But the easiest and best yet: help us spread the word.
– Talk to your department about HackDay
– Blog about it
– let your social networks know
– talk to management or DEs or VPs to get their sponsorship
– have them become judges for you local event.
