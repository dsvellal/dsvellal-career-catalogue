## Context
Continuation of Datta's advisory conversation with Surendhar Vaithiyanathan (PSO for ISPACS/IDM) about the IDM team's plan to use K3S (lightweight Kubernetes) for their NEB Node OS upgrade. Surendhar raised concerns about K3S not being whitelisted by CAO (Central Architecture Office) and lifecycle management challenges. Datta engaged with the concern, asking about self-hosted Kubernetes and querying whether CAO actively maintained their software lists. Surendhar pointed to SSD COE as responsible but said it needed verification. This part is a direct evidence of Datta's probing, technically-grounded engagement with governance and compliance concerns.
