## Content

**Post by Vieira, Fernando — Mon at 2:07 PM (4 shares, seen by 487)**

**State of Software Craftsmanship 2023**

> The Software Craftsmanship program has been pivotal to leverage software best practices in Philips over the last years. Data collected from the program's enrolled teams represents over 80% of the software community. We analyze the data and generate KPIs; those KPIs are available in our dashboards shared with team leaders.
>
> In 2023 we decided to go deep into data analytics to get new insights and draw more conclusions. As an outcome of this effort, we are happy to present the State of Software Craftsmanship 2023 report. In the document, you will find facts and numbers about development teams and the current maturity of the most popular software practices in Philips.
>
> The summarized findings and strategic advice aim to guide leaders & practitioners in prioritizing efforts to reach excellence. The Software Excellence team is happy to work with you to make this happen.
>
> The report is available in our Share Point and can be accessed here.
