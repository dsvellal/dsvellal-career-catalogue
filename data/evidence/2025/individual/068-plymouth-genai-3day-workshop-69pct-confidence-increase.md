### Title
> **Ending 2025 on a great learning note!**

### Narrative
> I was in Plymouth, Minnesota, invited by our **IGTD team**, to deliver an **in-person hands-on GenAI workshop** for three days, the 16th, 17th, and 18th of December 2025.
>
> We explored GenAI tools, prompt engineering, context engineering, specification-driven development, requirement management leveraging AI, GitHub and agent support in GitHub & CoPilot, and the AI support offered by Philips.
>
> Through these hands-on workshops, we were able to achieve both high satisfaction scores and, more importantly, **a measurable transformation in workforce readiness**. The feedback confirms that we successfully **shifted the team's mental model from viewing AI as a "chatbot" to leveraging it as an "engineering partner,"** and we have to maintain momentum to build on this.
>
> Imagine my delight when our IGTD colleague sent me a note mentioning that they leveraged spec-driven development to build a game that they'll use to play with a family member! Another colleague mentioned that they are now less hesitant to explore AI tools; another colleague built a tool in under 20 minutes leveraging AI to shave off an hour or so of repetitive manual excel sheet work! **All real values were achieved after this workshop!**

### Key Insights (QUANTIFIED)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| **Satisfaction Score** | **~8.9/10** average across all three days |
| **Confidence increase** | **69%** in leveraging AI for daily tasks |
| Pre-Workshop Confidence | **4.5/10** (Tentative usage) |
| Post-Workshop Confidence | **7.6/10** (Active application) |

### Strategic Insights

1. **The "Aha!" Moment:** The most significant engagement didn't come from basic coding features but from **Spec-Driven Development** and **Agentic AI** workflows. The team is hungry for structured "thinking" agents rather than just autocomplete tools.

2. **Methodology Adoption:** The **"TOON" Prompting Framework** was widely cited as a key takeaway, giving engineers a structured way to control AI output rather than guessing.

3. **Trainer Perception:** Feedback consistently described the facilitation as **"passionate" and "inclusive."** A recurring theme was appreciation for the patience shown in ensuring **"no one was left behind,"** bridging the gap between our tech elites and the broader engineering team.
