### Title

### Published
March 13, 2025

### The Story
Datta volunteered as a coach for his children's **Math Pentathlon** team. He became a bottleneck when parents repeatedly asked him rules questions during games, causing delays.

### The Innovation
He fed Google's **NotebookLM** (an AI tool that analyzes and synthesizes information from multiple sources) with Math Pentathlon resources:
- PDFs, videos, documents, rules

Created a **conversational AI assistant** that parents could query directly:
> e.g., "Can I move five spaces in FIAR?" → instant answer

This eliminated dependency on Datta and allowed games to flow without delays — benefiting **dozens of families**.

### Broader Applications Extrapolated

3. **Interactive educational tutoring**
4. **Healthcare guidance systems**
5. **Event management coordination**
