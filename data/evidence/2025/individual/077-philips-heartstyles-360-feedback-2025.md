## Assessment Framework

HeartStyles measures character through 4 quadrants:
1. **Personal Growth (Blue)** — Developing self, learning, humility
2. **Growing Others (Red)** — Encouraging, developing others, serving
3. **Self Promoting (Green)** — Competing, achieving, driving results
4. **Self Protecting (Orange)** — Controlling, avoiding, defending

The assessment uses 75 behavioral phrase items rated by self and nominated respondents. Results are presented as percentile scores compared to global norms.
