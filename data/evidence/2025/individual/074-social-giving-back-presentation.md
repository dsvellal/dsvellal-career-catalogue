## Summary

This is NOT a professional tech talk or corporate CSR initiative. This is a deeply personal 39-slide photo album documenting Datta's GRASSROOTS SOCIAL SERVICE spanning approximately 2007 to 2015. The presentation captures 8+ years of volunteering with underprivileged children at government schools in Karnataka, teaching yoga to children and adults, distributing hundreds of notebooks and school supplies to rural schools, coaching exam preparation and essay writing, conducting science demonstrations, and organizing cultural programs.

This is personal, self-funded, family-supported social work — performed alongside (and even before) his professional career at IBM and later roles. The final image (slide 39) shows Datta with his wife holding their infant baby, surrounded by 40+ underprivileged children at a free residential school — proving this is a FAMILY commitment to social service, not an individual side project.
