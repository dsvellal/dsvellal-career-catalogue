>
> **Here's what we did:**
> 🎬 Took a **grooming team's KT video transcript** of an existing service for a new release.
> 📝 Used **GitHub Copilot** to **generate user stories**, ensuring all mandatory characteristics like description, acceptance criteria, positive/negative scenarios, and validations were covered.
> ✅ From there, we further leveraged Copilot to **derive complete functional test cases** with detailed steps, validations, and conditions
>
> This experiment has now opened up a **new horizon** 📂 for us to explore **Test-Driven Development (TDD)** ✅ and further integrate GenAI into our engineering and QA workflows.
>
