## Datta's Full Comment (verbatim)

> Did you know you can ask the AI to become your master prompt engineer?
>
> **Secret tip: ask the AI to generate the prompt to do the task instead of asking it to do the task - and see the difference!**
>
> Here's my favorite hack:
> - Imagine a scenario where you want to create a report based on <sources>, and this report is intended for senior team members (like engineers, leads, managers, directors, or above).
>
> 👉 Instead of asking the AI to directly generate the report with boring prompts like so:
> *Create an executive report based on the <sources>.*
>
> Ask the AI to **become aware** of and **interpret the context** of a situation and **create powerful prompts** first.
>
> Here's one of the ways of doing it:
>
> *I am <insert-your-role>. I intend to create a report to be presented to <insert-audience-roles> based on <provide-your-sources>. In this report, I want to ensure that I include <specify-topics-that-are-of-interest>. The generated report should be in a <formal-or-casual-or-executive-or-academic-or-friendly> tone and look similar to <provide-a-report-that-you-want-this-generated-report-to-look-like>.*
>
> *Can you give me <insert-a-number> of the best prompts based on this context so I can use them to generate the report?*
>
> Provide the prompts in a table with three columns:
> 1. **The prompt itself**
> 2. **How this prompt helps generate the report I need**
> 3. **Why the prompt is structured that way**
>
> Once you've got your shiny new list of prompts, choose your adventure:
> - 🎯 Pick the one you like best, tweak it, and get your dream report.
> - 🎨 Use two or more (even all) of the prompts to create multiple report versions, then mix and match the best parts to create a masterpiece.
> - 🧠 Pro tip: let the AI handle the heavy lifting for you at each step!
>
> 🌟 **SHINE BRIGHT, MY PROMPT ENGINEER! SHINE BRIGHT!** 🌟

## The Meta-Prompting Framework (Datta's method)

| Step | Action |
|------|--------|
| 1 | Don't ask AI for the output directly |
| 2 | Provide context: your role, audience, sources, interests, tone, format |
| 3 | Ask AI to generate multiple PROMPTS for the task |
| 4 | Request table: prompt / how it helps / why it's structured that way |
| 5 | Choose best prompt(s), tweak, and generate final output |
| 6 | Mix-and-match multiple versions for a masterpiece |
