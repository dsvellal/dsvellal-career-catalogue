## Post Content (verbatim)

### Title
> **Compliance check is now natively supported on Kairos!**

### Opening
> Are you concerned about your software requirements not adhering to the **IEC 62304 Ed 1.1 standard** or the **FDA's Premarket Submission for Device Software Functions Guidance** document?
>
> Do you want to review your release documents (URS, PRS, SRS, SSRS, etc.) and identify any potential compliance gaps with the aforementioned standard and guidance, including **INCOSE guidance**?
>
> ***The prompt to do so is now natively available on Kairos!***

### How to Use (7 steps)
1. Go to Kairos: https://frontend-3ie637jzaqqru.azurewebsites.net/
2. Create a new chat.
3. Upload your requirement document(s) in the documents section of the chat.
4. Switch back to "Chat" section
5. Click on the "predefined prompts" icon on the bottom left corner of the chat box.
6. Go all the way down and select the "IEC62304 and FDA PMS Compliance Review" prompt.
7. Once you do this, a predefined prompt will be added to the chat box. Hit "enter" or the ">" arrow key on the right.

### Value Statement
> Watch the magic happen as the prompt now **progressively identifies your requirements** in the document(s) uploaded, **runs the rules on each of them**, and gives you **potential compliance gaps**!
>
> Read more about the use cases of extending Kairos to work on your requirements in this article: **ReqSpec: From Vague to Verifiable Requirements.**

### Key Details
> **The best part is that you do not need a license to use Kairos!** Kairos is developed by **Engelblik, Bas**, and his team from the PQR community, and is a safe space for us to leverage AI on Philips-specific documents.
