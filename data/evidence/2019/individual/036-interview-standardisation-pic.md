Dear All,

Greetings from the PIC TA team!

Some of you would recall the request from the TA team late last year, related to your support on setting up a PIC Hiring committee of experienced and skilled  interviewers to improve the overall Interview Quality across PIC and drive standardisation. This team(we expect to add more names to this list as the initiative kicks off) would be the go-to resources for setting and improving the interview quality and standards across PIC as we seek to hire the best external technology talent from Digital companies. Those who haven’t, will get a brief on the initiative when we have a meeting with Srinivas  Prasad and the MT members

As a first step towards this, we would like to organize a meeting with Prasad and some of the  PIC  MT members to understand this program in finer detail and so also to get your thoughts and suggestions  - to put a structure to the  process of interview standardization at PIC. The goal is to  raise the overall interview standards and engagement levels of our interview process – which is becoming a  key talent attraction tool in digital skills hiring.

One of the steps we have taken towards upgrading our processes has been to introduce online Assessments(Hackerearth) for coding heavy roles(@CG 50 and below) but there are several areas that we need to augment and improve in parallel in order to benchmark with the best tech majors in the industry.

As next steps, you can expect an invite from Sophia for a meeting with Prasad and team to set context and way forward on this critical initiative.

Do kindly reach out to Ramu, Nitin or myself directly if you have any specific queries regarding this initiative, ahead of the meeting.

Thanks & Regards,
