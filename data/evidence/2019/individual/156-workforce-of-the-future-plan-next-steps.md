Hi All,



Philips has a vision to shape a lean and agile workforce, which has capabilities to drive our business strategy , leverage economies of scale, emerging technologies, and total workforce possibilities. As we move towards shaping our PIC Vision 2025,  it is important to also carve out what would be our “Workforce of the future”.



As a part of that, the HR team did a study where they interacted with many stakeholders within PIC, studied the SWP for various business groups and also interacted with some external consultants to arrive at 5 strategic imperatives for PIC . For each imperative, the need is to understand its talent implications, create a charter/ action plan to achieve them and come out with measurable KPIs. A team has been put in place to work on each of them



The HR leads in each group will anchor the discussions and help you come out with recommendations. You will be required to present them in a workshop which I am planning to conduct tentatively in the last week of July/ 1st week of Aug. In the workshop we should be able to collectively define a charter for our “Workforce of the future” and if we as leadership feel that there is any change to be made to the direction of this initiative, let’s do so
