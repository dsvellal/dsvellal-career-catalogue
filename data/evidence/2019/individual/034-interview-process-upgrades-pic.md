Following some of our earlier discussions on this topic, some of the action points for bringing in the Bar raiser and debrief steps into our interview process is outlined below:

Suggested Process Design for pilots(MR/CT/SIG/SW COE BUs):


1.       Phone Screen by TA (15-20 mins) + Online Technical assessment (Hackerearth) up to CG 60        (90 mins)

2.       Technical Interview 1 – remote (via Phone and using collaboration tools such as collabedit)         (45 mins)

3.       Interview day: Technical interview 2 + Manager Round - On site with Programming test case.     (90 mins interview time plus lunch break and team member catch-up sessions)

4.       Bar-raiser round                                                                                                                                  (45 mins)

5.       De-brief call to arrive at Hire/No-Hire decision                                                                                 (20-30 mins)


Callouts:


·         As part of strategic capabilities initiative, it has been recognized that the current interview process needs to be augmented to match best practices in the Digital Industry.

·         The attempt is to start work on a “Bar Raiser” model to begin with and bring in a “De-Brief” round into the process to rely more on “wisdom of crowds” than on individual decisions. The Hiring committee model may be considered at a later stage once the overall process/interviewers have matured to a certain higher level of proficiency.

·         The technical and/or Manager roud may incorporate the competency assessment framework that is a product of the strategic capabilities workshop.

·         The next steps would be to identify potential Bar raisers within the campus. This may be led by the BU leaders with help from TA/HR based on prior interview data and on-the-job performance

·         We would request The SW COE team led by Rani/Datta/Sundaresan to help train the selected Bar Raisers and possibly certify them as future ambassadors for the Interview processes at PIC. certification model to identify/assess/train and certify the best interviewers may be a great pull factor for the program

·         One of the critical callouts during the initial meetings for the bar raiser model to succeed, is that  it requires the technical interviewer to be hands-on with coding, with the ability to extensively document the interview events and give logical recommendations basis the interview notes. Examples of technical interviews conducted by one of the potential bar raisers (Dattatreya, whom we recently hired from Amazon) is attached.
