## Key Contributions Evidenced

### 1. Authored Standardisation Framework (Feb 2019)
From Datta's email proposing the initiative:
> "I am just jotting down the points that we may want to consider when standardising the interview process."

Five-point proposal:
1. Evaluating the evaluator after every round via online feedback survey
2. SWAG for interviewers (laptop stickers, hoodies, pens)
3. Recognition/awards/thank-yous for interviewers
4. Training sessions for interviewers (eligibility to interview @ Philips)
5. Shadow/Reverse-Shadow procedures for continuous improvement

### 2. Conducted "How to Interview" Training (May 2019)
- Delivered 1hr 15min session to DXR team
- Covered: collecting data-points, question design, maintaining neutrality
- Created feedback survey to measure session effectiveness
- Received formal Recognition Card from the team lead

### 3. Led Hiring Process Meetings
