It's been insightful to see how the development cycle and the deployment cycle function as an integral system and how it increases the speed of innovation.
We can benefit from this way of working in our research programs. During the investigation phase we can involve our co-create partners at an early stage.
Another great insight - often cited as the Tesla example- is that is not a one-time release. You deliver a framework and keep advancing after release to shorten the time to market.

- How does it support our Innovation 2025 transformation?

It is essential to build software and informatics capabilities and increase the speed of innovation.
