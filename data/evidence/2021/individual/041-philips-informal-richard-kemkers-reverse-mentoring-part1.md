**Q: Why did you decide to join the Reverse Mentoring program?**

**Q: What is the best insight you got so far?**
> **It's been insightful to see how the development cycle and the deployment cycle function as an integral system and how it increases the speed of innovation.**

> Another great insight — often cited as the Tesla example — is that it is not a one-time release. You deliver a framework and keep advancing after release to shorten the time to market.

**Q: How does it support our Innovation 2025 transformation?**
> It is essential to build software and informatics capabilities and increase the speed of innovation.

**Q: What can be improved?**
> The online meeting are working well. We adjusted our bi-weekly meetings to monthly sessions of an hour, and I would encourage others to make the time as well. This allows for a deeper conversation.
