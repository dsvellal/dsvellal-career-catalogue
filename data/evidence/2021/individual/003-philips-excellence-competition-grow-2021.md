### 2. Technical Engagement of Software Leaders

### 3. Learning Organization

## Original Gaps Addressed
1. Software roles/KARs were undefined and inconsistently mapped
2. No way to identify or reach the software community
3. No dedicated development program for first-line SW leaders
4. No high-quality technical learning resources
5. Collaboration siloed within teams/BUs
6. SW role models had limited visibility channels
7. Talent hired for business not company
8. Quality of hire varied across organization
9. Feedback collected inconsistently
