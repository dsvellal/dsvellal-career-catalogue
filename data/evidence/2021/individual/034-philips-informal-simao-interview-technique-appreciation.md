## Context

Simao Williams (Datta's manager/senior colleague) sent unsolicited positive feedback after a joint interview session. Simao had shadow-observed Datta conducting a technical interview and provided specific, detailed feedback on what made Datta's interview style exceptional. Simao also referenced the value of Datta's approach beyond just interviews — noting it as a general work trait. He suggested adding Datta's reverse-shadow approach to Philips' Bar Raisers Program.

## Content

**Williams, Simao (Tuesday 1:51 pm):**
> "Yes fine. Reverse shadow would be good for interview training & sharing in general I think across Philips maybe to add to bar raisers program. Sometimes managers do not realise how good some of their senior engineers are at interviewing far more than just technical (they just use technical topics to better understand the candidate personality, comms skills, courage, creativity, etc.)"

**Williams, Simao (Tuesday 1:53 pm):**
> "btw, some positive feedback.... one thing I really like about your interview style is that you give just the right amount of time & space for your candidate to show what they can do but without over-running and still covering most of what you wanted to know. I often interrupt the candidate's thoughts or work too early in my impatience to learn more about them. Summary: I feel/look fast in interview but I am actually slowing myself down in getting full quality output. Your pace looks slower but gets the right outcome. -- this is my big learning from watching you in general (not only in interview). Nice traits!"

**Williams, Simao (Tuesday 1:56 pm):**
> "......only bonus for me going faster and interrupting candidate with many rapid-fire questions is that we get to see how they work under pressure -- but on balance much prefer your style"
