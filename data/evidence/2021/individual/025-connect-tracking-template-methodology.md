## Template Structure

### Sheet 1: Summary
A structured dashboard with 20 key metrics computed monthly:

| # | Metric | Description |
|---|--------|-------------|
| 1 | Working days with connects | Days that had at least one .connect session |
| 2 | Total working days | Datta's working days that month |
| 3 | Connect-to-working-day ratio | Ratio of days with connects to total days (formula) |
| 4 | No. of connects | Total count of program connects |
| 5 | Per-day connects | Average connects per day with connects (formula) |
| 6 | Avg. minutes per connect | Mean session duration (formula) |
| 7 | Connects setup by Datta | Proactively initiated by Datta |
| 8 | Connects setup by Others | Demand-driven / requested by colleagues |
| 9 | Total minutes spent | Total time investment |
| 10 | Total hours spent | Converted from minutes (formula) |
| 11 | Total colleagues met | All participant touchpoints |
| 12 | Colleagues in Bangalore | Primary location engagement |
| 13 | Colleagues rest-of-world | Global engagement (formula) |
| 14 | Total unique colleagues | Distinct individuals engaged |
| 15 | Total cities covered | Geographic diversity count |
| 16 | Top 5 cities (outside Bangalore) | Geographic reach indicators |
| 17 | Top 5 job roles covered | Seniority and role diversity |
| 18 | No. of departments covered | Organizational breadth |
| 19 | Interviews taken for SWCoE | Recruitment contribution |
| 20 | .tune sessions conducted | Observation/coaching sessions |

### Sheet 2: UniqueParticipants
- Pivot table of all participant emails with frequency count
- Enables tracking of most-engaged colleagues and repeat interactions

### Sheet 3: UniqueCities
- Pivot table of participant cities with frequency count
- Tracks geographic distribution of engagement

### Sheet 4: NormalizedJobTitle
- Pivot table of job titles with frequency count
- Shows seniority level distribution (Architect, Manager, Director, etc.)

### Sheet 5: NormalizedDepartment
- Pivot table of departments with frequency count
- Shows cross-organizational reach (PD, HSP, Q&R, etc.)

### Sheet 6: Monthly_DSVConnects (Raw Data)
- Complete meeting log with:
  - Meeting Start Date
  - Subject
  - Organizer
  - Duration (Minutes)
- Source data for all metrics calculations

### Sheet 7: NonDotConnectMeetingDetails
- Other essential meetings (not classified as program connects)
- Used to calculate the "% time on other meetings" metric
- Enables comparison of connect time vs. operational meeting time

## Methodology Design Principles

1. **Quantified engagement:** Every interaction measured by duration, participants, location, role, department
2. **Demand vs. supply tracking:** Separate tracking of Datta-initiated vs. others-initiated connects reveals demand signal
3. **Geographic diversity:** City-level tracking shows global reach and remote engagement capability
4. **Organizational breadth:** Department and role tracking proves cross-functional impact
5. **Time investment accountability:** Hours spent on connects vs. other meetings enables ROI discussions
6. **Unique vs. total:** Distinguishing unique colleagues from total touchpoints shows both depth and breadth
7. **Month-over-month comparability:** Standardized template enables trending and pattern recognition
