## Recommended Coping Mechanisms

1. **Strive to help other people** — combat self-indulgence by focusing outward
2. **Reinforce positive thinking** — 3 things grateful for daily
3. **Practice Mindfulness** — slow down, observe, shift mental gears
4. **Build a supportive network** — open up, talk about feelings
5. **Keep a diary/journal** — express anger constructively, identify triggers
6. **Practice relaxation skills** — 10-15 min daily deep breathing
