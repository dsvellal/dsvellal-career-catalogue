## Analysis
- **Recurring positive themes:** Interactivity (mentioned 12/19 times), engagement, practical content, STAR model teaching, good facilitation
- **Recurring improvement themes:** Session length (too long for some), clarity on next steps/follow-up, desire for more hands-on exercises
- **Program reach:** 7 cohorts trained over 7 months across multiple global sites — indicates ongoing demand for the program
- **Key teaching content:** STAR interview model, behavioral assessment, bar raiser methodology, interviewing biases
