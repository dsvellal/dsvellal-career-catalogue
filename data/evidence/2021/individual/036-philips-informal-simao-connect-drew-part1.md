## Context

On 24 June 2021, Simao Williams messaged Datta to let him know he was about to have a 1-2-1 with Drew (a senior leader in the USA) and offered to proactively advocate for Datta for a USA Competency Lead role. Simao framed this as an opportunity to tell Drew about Datta's talent before Datta reached out himself. This is the first part of a three-part exchange (see also 037 and 038).
