Carefully, look for contradictions -- an important indicator of integrity."

## 3. Changing the Hiring Culture (PDF, authored by Datta)

**Title:** Changing the hiring culture
**Author:** Subramanya Vellal, Dattatreya (Software Excellence Competency Lead)

### Opening Questions
> *"How to get the best software talent? and How do we provide the best experience for them?"*

### Program Description
> "We are moving away from the traditional techniques, to a more engaged conversation, data-driven feedbacks, quantitative analysis of the discussions and consensus driven hiring decisions."

### Candidate Journey (5 stages)
1. **Candidate identification & screening** — Done by talent acquisition team
2. **Pre-selection** — Technical assessment using HackerEarth combined with phone pre-screening
3. **Interview day** — Structured technical and behavioral interviews, bar-raiser round
4. **Hiring decision** — Structured feedback being collected, data-based decision making
5. **Offer** — Offer made by talent acquisition

### Key Approach Points
- **Structured interviewing:** Combination of behavioural interviews, pair working, problem-solving exercises, sample exercises, video materials prepared in cooperation with Software Excellence
- **Structured feedback & decision-making:** Forms and test scores compiled together as summary. Hiring decision based on data, bar raisers have right to input the final decision
- **Bar raisers' community:** Objective third party, driving consensus within the hiring loop
- **Community based hiring:** "hiring software engineers not for a specific team, but for Philips"

### Why an Upgraded Approach?
- Enhance Quality of Hires and minimize wrong hiring decisions
- Standardize the interview process across PIC — "Hire for Philips" with neutral third-party view
- Position Philips as "Great Place to Interview" with enhanced candidate experience
- Reduce time to convey hire/no-hire decision

### Goals
- Collect feedback in sustainable digital manner (Avature, GDPR compliant)
- Gatekeeping rights for recruiters to push back feedback
- Clear guidance for hiring teams on do's and don'ts
- Structured transparent framework enabling great candidate experience

### Tech Leveraged
- **Avature CRM** for Talent Pipelines
- **HackerEarth** assessments for all CG 50 and below hires
- **AI sourcing tools** and boolean search strings for LI Recruiter
- All materials included in .grow section of Software Excellence SharePoint

### Measuring Impact
- HVT hire percentage: target 23% (up from 20% in 2019)
- HM and candidate NPS to exceed industry benchmarks (HM 65%, Candidate 70%)
- Feedback from hiring managers on candidates
- Business impact parameters

### De-brief Structure
- First 5 minutes: Exchange interview notes
- Each panelist shares individual hire/no-hire decision with data
- Consensus-driven final decision
