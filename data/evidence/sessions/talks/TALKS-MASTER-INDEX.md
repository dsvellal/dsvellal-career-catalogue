## NOTABLE HIGHLIGHTS

### 1. US Patent US20120150789A1 (2012)

- **Title:** "Determining and Conveying User Availability"
- **Patent Number:** US 2012/0150789 A1
- **Filed:** December 10, 2010
- **Published:** June 14, 2012
- **Inventors:** Albee Jhoney (IN), Umasuthan Ramakrishnan (IN), **Dattatreya S. Vellal (IN)**
- **Assignee:** International Business Machines Corporation, Armonk, NY
- **Application No.:** 12/965,142
- **Int. Cl.:** G06N 5/04
- **Abstract:** Determining and conveying availability information by combining availability intent, desire, and inferred availability state into a composite availability state conveyed to a remote server.
- **Significance:** Published US patent while at IBM India Software Labs. Demonstrates innovation capability and patent-filing process mastery (later taught in IP talks).

### 2. IEEE Paper — Visual Component Dependency Management (2009)

- **Title:** "Visual Component Dependency Management"
- **Author:** Dattatreya S Vellal, India Software Labs, IBM India Pvt. Ltd. (davellal@in.ibm.com)
- **Venue:** 2009 AP QSE Symposium, Beijing, September 8-10
- **Organization:** WPLC, SWG - India (IBM)
- **Keywords:** Dependency Management, GUI, Eclipse Modelling Framework (EMF), Graphical Modelling Framework (GMF), Simulation
- **Abstract:** A "Dependency Management Tool" using MVC architecture and customized UML Model to visualize and manage component dependencies via rich GUI. Working prototype in Java/Eclipse.
- **Significance:** Technical paper presented at international symposium in Beijing within 2 years of joining IBM. Shows early research inclination and international exposure.
