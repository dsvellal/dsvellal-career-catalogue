with the team onshore as well as inside. The junior team, I believe sees him as a role model.
Knowledge:
On the knowledge front, Datta continues to grow his OneGate knowledge. Through his participation in architecture team he has become the
key player in re-architecting the OneGate product. He has demonstrated excellent skills in co-ordination as well as leading junior team
members. He could get better by being some more conservative in planning/estimation(or balancing the risk response) and understanding

to continue building practical technical, design, engineering process depth. We will work together to flesh these dimensions out as
expectations going forward.
Overall Datta has done exceedingly well in contributing to the success of the team. He has to continue to build on his strengths of execution,
leadership, communication and add to domain and technical skills.
