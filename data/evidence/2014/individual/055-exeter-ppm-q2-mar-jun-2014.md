to the effectiveness of the work done by them.

really well by refining the technical skills & domain knowledge to complement his execution & leadership skills. He would be the best all
rounder that team would have.
Overall Datta has done exceedingly well in contributing to the success of the team. He has to continue to build on his strengths of execution,
