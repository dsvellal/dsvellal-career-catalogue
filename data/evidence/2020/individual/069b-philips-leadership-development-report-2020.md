## Executive Summary

> "A customer focused and data driven individual, throughout the development center you demonstrated your desire to add value for the customers of Philips. Your data orientation enables you to take a well-informed and confident approach to the challenges and opportunities you face. This is further supported by your highly accountable approach, which sees you proactively identifying areas where you can add greater value by challenging the status quo. You demonstrate a genuine desire to take personal responsibility and ownership for driving improvement and positive change. Furthermore, your significant strength in the area of being articulate enables you to effectively break down complexity for others, and to create a safe and open space to have conversations."

## Development Areas Noted

- Risk of overusing articulate strength — "your approach to more senior stakeholders can trigger surprising responses"
- "You could benefit from further leveraging your reflective approach"
- "Your ability to break down complexity into simple steps can at times be a barrier to you connecting with others on the bigger picture"
- "You may be at risk of overusing your focus on taking responsibility, which can result in you taking ownership for things you could empower others to do"

## Key Development Objectives (from assessors)

1. Formulate and articulate a story/game plan connecting big picture with granular actions
2. Approach difficult conversations by challenging with data without being perceived as judgmental
3. Be aware of risks of overusing articulate strength; allow others space to bring their perspective
