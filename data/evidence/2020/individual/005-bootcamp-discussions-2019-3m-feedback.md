The summary of our discussions are:

 

The biggest change from 2019 will be- 'get it right at the start'.

The highlights in the 2020 sequence are:

1.	Language paradigms at the start: OO, Aspect-oriented, Functional+Lambda-calculus
	Exercises like "code without ifs" to expose the 'edge' of each paradigm
2.	Followed by Behavior ('I will code' session from Datta)
3.	Agile training + practice = daily learning-outcomes and deliverables: Exploration & acceptance-criteria
4.	Setup IDE with linting, duplication and complexity limits
5.	Demo to BU-rep to build trust before joining
6.	Assessment flavor: Introspection and teaching
7.	Professional skills to include Story writing (customer and technology-oriented, including NFRs)
8.	Overall bonus-target: Innersource a utility
9.	Identify & appreciate the thinkers/coaches who help other participants

Let me know any other thoughts that come to your mind.

 

Bye,
