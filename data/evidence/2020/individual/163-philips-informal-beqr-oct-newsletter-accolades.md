## Context
The BEQR October 2020 Newsletter (dated Oct 10, 2020) included an "Accolades" section specifically calling out the SWCoE team members — with Datta listed first — for their performance at the 1st SWCoE Virtual Face-to-Face (F2F) conference. Over 100 people from different businesses globally joined the virtual event. The recognition was for demonstrating "Eager to Improve and Inspire" and "Team Upto Win" behaviors.
