I wanted to share my experiences about the Bar raiser round which I recently had with Philips Panel.

It was indeed a great experience (first of this kind in my IT career).  Just like any interview with leadership I thought this would be just asking about my career progression and interests, but it was way beyond my expectation.

 

The discussion was very interactive and I never felt I was interviewed. It all started with an introduction about us and went on about my leadership styles.

 

In fact, the questions were completely based on situations I deal with on a day to day basis and it gave me a sense of feeling that my work was more valued. I was evaluated based on my previous and present experience , also on my working style and if it matches Philips working culture. I was assessed on my communication, knowledge of industry, my expectations, my vision and what as an Individual can contribute to the organization.

 

 

I understand this is an interview conducted from a different department and it is  really  worth to be accessed if I'm qualified not only for a particular team but for Philips as a whole. 

 

My sincere suggestion would be to continue these kinds of interviews in future for other candidates as well which will help both the candidates as well as the organization.
