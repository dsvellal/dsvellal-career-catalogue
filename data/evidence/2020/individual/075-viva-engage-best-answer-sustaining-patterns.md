**Full answer (verbatim):**
> Very nice question David. Thank you. You are absolutely right, its human tendency to move back to the steps that we were most comfortable with (until we get comfortable with new ways). To break away from this, we are adopting different techniques:
>
> 1. **Observe after 3-ish months** to see if patterns which, the observer and the observee agreed that, when eliminated, added a lot of value, is noticed again? If yes, then we have appropriate conversation with the relevant parties to understand how they'd want to take it forward.
>
> 2. **See if there are opportunities for automation** for such patterns, if yes, how can we partner and help the team in achieving it so that it is eliminated throughout.
>
> 3. **Share anonymized common patterns** with relevant leaders of the org and explain what actions can be taken to support eliminating a few of them.
>
> 4. **Groom and help grow many observers** within the organization so that dependency on SWCoE reduces, and the culture of observation & improvement continues in a self-sustained mode within the org.
>
> Except for the first one (we haven't completed 3 months yet!), remaining techniques are currently being adopted in some form and shape with different orgs who have participated in the **60+ observations we have done so far.**
