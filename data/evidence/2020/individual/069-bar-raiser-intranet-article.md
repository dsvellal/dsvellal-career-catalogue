## What the Article Documents

### The Philips Bar Raiser Program
An official company-wide intranet article documenting the launch and success of the Philips Bar Raiser Program — modeled after Amazon's Bar Keeper/Bar Raiser program (started 1999). Published under the Chief Technology Office for global visibility.

### Program Milestones
1. **Initiation:** Identifying Bar Raisers — "lead by examples, very passionate about people, always go an extra mile... influencers and 'go to' persons"
2. **Preparation:** Mandatory STARR training — structured interviews drawing concrete examples from past behaviors
3. **Experimentation:** 40+ Bar Raiser interviews conducted; expanded to US, EMEA, and Blumenau
