## Content

### Mindmap Structure: "Ideal Campus Interview Experience"

The mindmap is organized into three main branches:

#### Branch 1: Pre-placement Talk & Context Setting

**Sub-branches:**
- **Vision** — Introducing "Touching 3 billion lives by 2030"
- **Mission** — Clarifying perceptions about "Philips" and what it does
- **Q&A** — [Optional] "What's your area of interest aligned to what Philips does, as explained in Pre-placement talk?"

**Presentation Format:**
- Consider reducing 1hr pre-placement talk to 30mins
- 20 mins of passionate talk about what's happening at Philips
- 10 mins for Q&A
- Tech-talk should always connect back to "How the trendy-topic is applied at Philips"

#### Branch 2: Interview

**Interviewer Introduction:**
- Ice-breaker to put candidate at ease — informal questions, reflecting points from resume, "why did I join Philips?"
- [Optional] What's your area of interest aligned to what Philips does?
- Expectations of a new-hire
- "What do you want to become in your life?" — provoke them in terms of panning out 30+ years of life to expectations of the candidate

**Core Philosophy — "Interviewing for Philips & not for team/technology":**
- Problem solvers
- Logical thinkers
- Quick learners, Unlearners & re-learners
- Shows strong traits of Philips' cultural behaviours
- Good programmers
- Be patient with the problem, be persuasive, and stick with the problem until it's solved

**Interview Format Experiments:**
- Applying concepts to solve a problem — try an open-book format for problem solving
- [Experiment] Provide hints & see if the candidate picks it up & builds on it to solve a problem
- [Experiment] Provides questions upfront. Provide options for the candidate to come to the interview prepared!
- [Experiment] Consider combining managerial & HR rounds [Saves time, we don't have a landing business, improves efficiency since both of these rounds are looking at similar data]

**Round Configurations — "Can we consider this based on the situation on the ground":**
- 2 technical & 1 Manager/HR
- 1 extended technical & 1 Manager/HR
- 1 technical & 1 Manager/HR
- 1 technical, 1 Manager/HR, 1 GD

**Group Discussion:**
- GD brings behavioral traits of articulation, good means of filtering
- GD topics: Wacky (non-conventional topics) OR Topics bringing out 5 Philips behaviours

#### Branch 3: Feedback

**Structured Feedback Process — "Lighter version of the following process":**
- What competency was evaluated
- What question was asked
- What answer was given
- What was your opinion based on the answer
- What's your rating
- What's your decision (Hire/No-Hire)
- Justify the decision

## Significance

This mindmap demonstrates Datta's ability to:

1. **Design end-to-end processes** — Created a complete framework covering pre-placement, interview, and feedback stages
2. **Strategic thinking on talent acquisition** — Aligned campus hiring with Philips' mission ("Touching 3 billion lives by 2030") and cultural values
3. **Innovation in hiring** — Proposed experimental approaches (open-book format, providing questions upfront, combining rounds) that challenge conventional interview practices
4. **Standardization at scale** — Designed a flexible framework with multiple round configurations adaptable to "the situation on the ground"
5. **Competency-based evaluation** — Structured feedback process emphasizing justified decisions and competency mapping
6. **Cultural alignment** — Emphasis on hiring for Philips (organization) rather than for specific teams/technologies, looking for cultural behaviours
7. **Candidate experience focus** — Ice-breakers, context setting, and candidate-friendly experiments show empathy in process design

This artifact directly connects to Datta's work on interview standardization (Bar Raiser program, interview training for global teams) and demonstrates thought leadership in campus recruitment methodology.
