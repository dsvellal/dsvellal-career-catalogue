## Context
Viswanath, Sreekanth posted in the PIC Bangalore Yammer group announcing the results of HackerNoon 2020 — a 100% virtual hackathon organized by the Developer Forum at IGT Bangalore. 20 teams submitted ideas, 10 reached the finals held on 24th July. Datta was one of 3 judges (along with Gernot Eggen and Saravanan Narayanasamy) who quizzed the 10 finalists. The post names Datta explicitly as a judge. Datta's reply (partially visible) expresses enthusiasm about the maturity of the ideas.

## Content

**Viswanath, Sreekanth — August 6 at 02:26 PM:**

To PIC Bangalore, Vellal, Dattatreya, Eggen, Gernot, Crowl, Cindy, Vijayachandran, Priya, Prabhu, Hareesh, Jaschke, Lena, Gv, Kalavathi, Balu, Sushma, Narayanasamy, Saravanan, Saxena, Ritesh, and Medhi, Kedar

Happy to share with you all that IGT Bangalore conducted Hackernoon 2020 where we saw 20 teams submitting their very interesting ideas.

A 100% virtual event, managed completely by the Developer Forum which was formed by a group of passionate techies.

The finals were held on 24th July and we had 3 judges - Dattatreya Vellal, Gernot Eggen & Saravanan Narayanasamy – quizzing the 10 finalists.

All ideas were winners, but the judges had to choose the top few as shown below.

Winners:
1. [Binding in Sense — Marut, Vinayak, Chethan Santosh]
2. [Linux self-upgrade using remote repository and Linux snapshots for recovery — Ritesh, Tarun, Udayan]
3. [Your Own Device Assistant — Praband]
Peoples Choice: [Create 3D image models that can be printed from medical images — Kiran, Aswin, Raghav, Santosh]
Special Mentions: [IGT Stack Overflow — Raja, Rashmi, Kishor, Nita] and [Insights, A dynamic centralized application for real-time system monitoring from live hospital data — Praband, Sauraj]
