## Context
This is a powerful testimony of the Quality at Desk program's impact on the ASP (Analytics Solution Platform) team. The team lead (Sajith) explicitly credits Datta and Aravind for enabling a "cultural change" — developers now treat code quality as a first-class concern with mandatory local gates before commits. The team went from zero quality gates to implementing compiler warnings, cyclomatic complexity, and code coverage gates in both local builds and Jenkins pipelines. They also reference SWCoE-maintained GitLab tooling that Datta's team created as reusable infrastructure.
