## Context

This is a Mentimeter word-cloud screenshot capturing live audience feedback at the end of a Community of Practice (COP) session attended by RTEs (Release Train Engineers) and Scrum Masters as part of the Tech Connectedness program. The session was on "Quality at Desk" (a quality-at-source initiative). The poll question was "Your Feedback on today's COP session."

Datta was involved in facilitating and delivering this session. The Mentimeter code shown (32 81 50 5) and the 11 active participants visible in the bottom-right indicate active, real-time audience engagement during the session.

The dominant word in the cloud — rendered in the largest font — is "very informative," indicating it was the most-submitted response. Other prominent feedback words reinforce the positive reception.
