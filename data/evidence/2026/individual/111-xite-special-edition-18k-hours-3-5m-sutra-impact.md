>
> **Problem:** Manual traceability and gap analysis in software verification and validation were time-consuming, error-prone, and fragmented across multiple documents and systems.
>
> **Solution & Implementation:** The Sutra platform automates traceability using AI and a graph database, connecting requirements, tests, and risks in real time.
