### Content
> **Inside XITE Special Edition: AI-Powered End-to-End Traceability!**
>
> Last week we wrapped up a dynamic Inside XITE session with **+400 attendees** (!) spotlighting one standout project: *AI-powered end-to-end traceability for software verification and validation!*
>
> **Problem:** Manual traceability and gap analysis in software verification and validation were time-consuming, error-prone, and fragmented across multiple documents and systems.
>
> **Solution & Implementation:** The Sutra platform automates traceability using AI and a graph database, connecting requirements, tests, and risks in real time. It enables one-click impact analysis, identifies gaps, and generates missing test cases, drastically reducing manual effort and onboarding time.
>
> **Impact:**
> - **90%+ improvement** in automated traceability
> - **30-40% faster** onboarding experience (from 3 weeks to 3 days)
> - **20% improvement** in end-to-end lifecycle efficiency (up to 50% in some cases)
