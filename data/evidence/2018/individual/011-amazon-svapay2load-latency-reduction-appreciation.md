Big customer win!
This launch has helped us bring down the fraud check latency for SVA Load & HFC orders by ~86% at p90 level and by
~89% at p99 level. Post 100% dial up of weblab on June 5th, we have seen a consistent drop of ~6.5 to 7 secs at P90 level
(moving from ~7.8 secs before we started Fortress dial up to ~1.09 secs post 100% dial up). At p99 level, the fraud check
latency came down from ~14.52 secs to ~1.58 secs – a drop of nearly 13 secs. This reducRon in latency was achieved while
keeping queue rates almost consistent.
Kudos to the team for diving deep on this and showing high ownership in driving mul>ple complex
changes.
