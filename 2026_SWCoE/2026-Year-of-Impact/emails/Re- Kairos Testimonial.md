🤖 AI On-Demand: Accelerating Requirements Compliance with the Kairos ReqSpec Prompt 🤖

Creating high-quality requirement specifications is often more difficult than it should be. Teams must translate ideas into clear, structured, and testable requirements while balancing business intent, user needs, quality expectations, and implementation realities. In practice, this often leads to ambiguity, incomplete scope, inconsistent quality, and unnecessary rework downstream.

Kairos has predefined prompts, one of which is: "IEC62304 and FDA PMS Compliance Review". This prompt helps teams draft stronger requirement specifications faster and with greater consistency.

By evaluating your existing requirements, and guiding users through a more structured way of defining context, scope, functional requirements, non-functional requirements, assumptions, dependencies, risks, and acceptance criteria, the prompt helps transform early requirement drafting from a fragmented activity into a more repeatable and effective process.

⚡With Kairos, teams can now:

•                Review existing requirements against expectations of IEC 62304, FDA Premarket Submission for Software Device Functions and INCOSE guidances.

•                Improve consistency and clarity across requirements

•                Reduce ambiguity and identify gaps earlier

•                Strengthen acceptance criteria and review readiness

•                Enable better alignment across business, product, engineering, and quality teams

Kairos doesn’t replace team expertise - it enhances it, helping teams spend less time wrestling with structure and wording, and more time improving requirement quality and delivery confidence.

Why it matters:

Round-the-clock AI support can improve First-Time-Right requirement drafting, reduce review cycles, and help teams move from vague inputs to actionable specifications more efficiently. Better requirements lead to better collaboration, better design decisions, and stronger execution outcomes.
