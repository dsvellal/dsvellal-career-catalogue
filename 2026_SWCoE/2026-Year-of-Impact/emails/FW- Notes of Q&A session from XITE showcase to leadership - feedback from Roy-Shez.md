AI-driven Troubleshooting Solution for Philips Ultrasound Factory
Question - Roy: We are facing this globally. What is the change in way of working that gets us to a different outcome?
Answer: The key change is not manual to digital; it is reactive troubleshooting to a scalable analysis system. We automate detection, data collection, and initial analysis, and speed up data-mining. Engineers spend their time validating solutions instead of searching for data.
Question - Shez: Are you going to do the demo in Reedsville to show the real impact of XITE?
Answer: Planned.
LAZURion: Image-Guided Coronary Laser Atherectomy
Question - Shez: How do you measure effectiveness and impact? Is it numerical or just qualitative, such as whether a customer is willing to pay for it?
Answer: A second round for the user interface will use quantitative measures.
OB Boxless
Question - Roy: With a 2.5-year development time, what is the biggest throughput time impacting the 2.5 years?
Answer: It is due to capacity planning and adhering to clinical process timelines.
Question - Roy: What differentiates this product from competitors' solutions?
Answer: It uses our cloud service and platform.
SiPM cost disruption high-end photon counting CT
Question - Shez: Is the cost savings a quantum leap?
Answer: This is an alternative, long-term opportunity, and the opportunity is too good for Philips not to look into. It is being used for low-cost CT systems.
Question - Roy: What is the quality delta?
Answer: Photon technology is approximately 30% of standard CT, with high potential to increase to 80%.
Question - Shez: Is it multi-energy?
Answer: Yes, everything can be done, but it needs to become faster with improved quality.
Cartesio - AI-Ready Content Intelligence
Question - Roy: Is this only for our own made content, or also third-party content?
Answer: Predominantly our own content, in collaboration with our partners.
Question - Shez: Is it proprietary to Google? Would it be possible to use it with other tooling?
Answer: It is our own content and within the Philips infrastructure.
AI for Customer Education Workflows
Question - Charlotte: What is the productivity improvement, as we are looking into the ROI of AI?
Answer: We measured the productivity improvement through comparison experiments, by comparing the results without AI versus the results achieved with AI.
Question - Charlotte: Are there scaling opportunities?
Answer: The technology has already been reviewed to be scaled across business units.
DioramAI Studio
Question - Shez: Are the digital assets, such as 3D, vectors, and CAD, based on what we already have, or do we need to build them?
Answer: We work with suppliers on CAD creation.
Question - Shez: Is this all virtual?
Answer: Yes, the project is using digital twins for everything.
AI Plaque Characterization
Question - Charlotte: What are the major competitor differentiators? Which of our competitors can or do this as well?
Answer: So far, Philips is the only one looking into this. The guidelines are fairly new.
Question - Roy: If we compare this with Lumify, are we looking at combining products and forces?
Answer: Yes, and Lumify would be a great landing spot.
Sutra: AI Powered V&V Compliance
Question - Roy: I liked it a lot. Are you including regulators in the development process?
Answer: By linking with Windchill, the rules can mimic regulators.
Question - Shez: When do we expect actual outcome data?
Answer: It is part of the current release cycles of the business units and will go live in December 2026.
Virtual Regulatory Expert - Regina AI
Question - Roy: How do you connect it with the global ecosystem beyond China?
Answer: The methodology for global deployment would be the same across the world.
GenAI Driven ISCV Test Modernization
Question - Shez: This build is tailored for ISCV. Can it be deployed in other business units for scaling, and when will you see and measure the impact?
Answer: The framework is included in Project Elevate, is scalable, and has delivered a 40% efficiency gain since February 2026.
Camera Based ECG-less Cardiac CT
Question - Charlotte: This connects to the ASTRA platform. What would be the go-to-market timing?
Answer: The features will be ready in the first phase to show robustness in clinical studies. In the second phase of ASTRA, it could be deployed into the platform.
NextGen Clinical Evaluation
