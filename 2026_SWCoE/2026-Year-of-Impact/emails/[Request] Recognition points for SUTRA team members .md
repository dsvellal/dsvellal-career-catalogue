Software Application Engineering

Ultrasound Fleet Manager – Skylight


Managing a fleet of ultrasound devices today means physically locating each machine, copying logs, walking between units, and manually compiling reports — repeated across every device in a hospital. Skylight replaces this with a single connected platform that gives biomedical engineers, clinical managers, and administrators centralised visibility into device inventory, utilisation insights, and remote configuration across their entire ultrasound fleet.




The IEN team — Fazil, Prateek, Tanisha, and Vidya — delivered full-stack engineering for the Skylight application, contributing approximately 85% of the Utilization module's development effort, building end-to-end observability, and driving the Pilot release to successfully onboard customers. AI-assisted development saved ~288 man-hours in a single PI, improving delivery capacity by 20%. The platform has scaled ASP data extraction from 125 to ~100,000 devices — and an RSNA demo drove adoption across three customer accounts, with one customer noting: "Skylight with the whole next generation of fleet management is actually becoming a game changer." NPS: 9/10.
