Why take advantage of this support?
Hackathons often move fast, making it tempting to jump straight into building. Having an experienced architect as a sounding board can help you focus on the most valuable problem to solve, avoid unnecessary complexity, and strengthen the overall quality of your solution. A short conversation can save significant effort later and help turn a good idea into a compelling and scalable one. The goal is not to evaluate your work, but to support your thinking, broaden perspectives, and help your team achieve the best possible outcome. Please note that the mentor is not a member of your team and is not responsible for the team's results or deliverables.
