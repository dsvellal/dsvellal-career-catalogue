Dear all,

I would like to follow up on the I&D initiative, Project Velocity. In Q4 last year, we have met on several occasions to dive into your business unit needs and journey to develop, deploy and scale AI solutions in the software development process.

After consolidating all gathered insights and careful consideration, we believe that AI enablement must balance enterprise scale with business-specific maturity, while ensuring governance, trust and tangible productivity gains. Therefore, the Innovation & Design Leadership team, backed by the R&D Leader Forum, decided the following:

Project Velocity will be put on hold. There will be no central, standardized R&D tooling initiative under Project Velocity at this stage to avoid duplication and preserve flexibility. We advise to build on the success of ongoing initiatives across I&D that aim to increase R&D efficiency and effectiveness in software development through AI-enabled workflows:

  *   Data & AI: Community of Practice AI (i.e. software development cross-business initiative) lead by Patrick Mans
  *   Innovation Engineering: Project Elevate (i.e. Software Modernization) lead by Chad Malone
  *   Research: XITE Special Edition (i.e. Project “AI Powered Compliance Solution”) lead by Betsabeh Madani Hermann

If you have any questions, please let me know.


Background
Project Velocity began exploring ways to address cross business unit R&D challenges by leveraging AI and an Enterprise Innovation approach to enhance R&D process efficiency. The team examined possibilities to reduce duplication, speed up delivery, and improve visibility of reusable assets within the PDLM software development process, focusing on use cases that meet multi-business unit AI innovation needs and scale validated outcomes across the enterprise.
