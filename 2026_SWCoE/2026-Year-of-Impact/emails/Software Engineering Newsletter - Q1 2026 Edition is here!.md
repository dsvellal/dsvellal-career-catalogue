XITE - AI Powered Compliance

The XITE AI Powered Compliance project demonstrates how Philips can unlock value by integrating enterprise data and applying AI to create end-to-end traceability across requirements, risks, and verification.
It enables advanced search, impact analysis, and identification of gaps between requirements and test coverage, with AI augmenting missing test scenarios.
Teams can interact conversationally with the data to uncover insights and potential compliance issues.
Built on the HSP AWS stack, it showcases how connected data and AI can enhance compliance, quality, and engineering effectiveness.
