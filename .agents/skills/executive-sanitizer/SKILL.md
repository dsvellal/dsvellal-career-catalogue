# Executive Sanitizer Skill

## Framing Principles
When transforming career artifacts into executive evidence, strictly enforce:
1. **Systemic Intervention Over Activity:**
   - Never write: "Managed team", "delivered project", "wrote code".
   - Always write: "Without intervention, X was projected to fail/stall. Chose high-stakes trade-off Y. Executed strategic intervention Z. Resulted in permanent systemic capability W."
2. **CAR / STAR Anatomy:**
   - `id`: unique kebab-case ID.
   - `title`: High-impact executive headline.
   - `scope`: Scope of responsibility (e.g., Global Multi-Site Org, 45+ Engineers, Critical Diagnostic Cloud).
   - `archetype`: e.g. "Systems Turnaround", "0-to-1 Scaler", "Organizational Architect".
   - `defaultTrajectory`: The failing status quo without intervention.
   - `strategicDecision`: The hard choice/trade-off made under uncertainty.
   - `intervention`: The precise systemic leverage point applied.
   - `observableConsequence`: Quantified, permanent outcome and business impact.
   - `verificationAnchor`: e.g., "Verified: Peer Feedback & Executive Endorsements".
   - `competencyTags`: searchable tags.
