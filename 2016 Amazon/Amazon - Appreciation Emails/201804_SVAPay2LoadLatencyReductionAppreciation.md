What has been launched?
We launched the Fortress Retail pipeline for ﬁrst set of IN GLs on 6/5. The launch was aimed
at SVA Load and High Frequency Category (HFC) GLs. Fortress Retail pipeline allows us to
evaluate orders for these GLs at signiﬁcantly reduced latencies of ~1sec at p90 level and
~1.6s at p99 level while preserving the quality of decisions.
Why is this important?
While customer trust is one of the most important pivots for a payments product, high load
latency results in poor customer experience and puts us at a disadvantage when compared
to other ICs. Owing to the very nature of these oﬀerings, a customer expects instant
fulﬁlment with the click of a bu\on and each second’s delay adds to their anxiety. The Pay to
Load latency for SVA was around 27secs at P90 level when SVA Team undertook a 2018 Goal
to reduce it to 4secs. Amongst other services, Fraud Check was iden>ﬁed as one of the
major contributors to this latency with P90 for Fraud Check Latency being ~8secs (and P99
being ~15secs). We, the fraud risk team, thereby, undertook a goal to reduce fraud check
latency to ~1sec for these categories at P90 level by leveraging the ‘Fortress Retail’ pipeline.
This was an audacious goal as (a) ‘Fortress Retail’ is a fairly new pipeline with only few
marketplaces using it, and (b) the marketplaces (BR, MX, US with 5% Dial Up) using ‘Fortress
Retail’ register P90 latencies in the range of 2-4 secs – well beyond our goal of 1 sec.
This project also helped us build intents speciﬁc to SVA Load. These intents now present us
with a unique opportunity to use them as the core evalua>on framework for various SVA
Load bol>ng projects that may get launched in the coming months. The project would also
serve as a precursor to inline evalua>on for SVA Load & HFC which will not only reduce
latency further but will also enable us to oﬀer risk based MFA, if required.
How are we measuring the success of this launch?
This launch has helped us bring down the fraud check latency for SVA Load & HFC orders by
~86% at p90 level and by ~89% at p99 level. Post 100% dial up of weblab on June 5th, we
have seen a consistent drop of ~6.5 to 7 secs at P90 level (moving from ~7.8 secs before we
started Fortress dial up to ~1.09 secs post 100% dial up). At p99 level, the fraud check
latency came down from ~14.52 secs to ~1.58 secs – a drop of nearly 13 secs. This reduc>on
in latency was achieved while keeping queue rates almost consistent.
