IMPORTANT: JIRA steps for developers

Team,
We are looking at JIRA for measuring the quality metrics and adhering strictly to the below steps will help us arrive at
the right quality metrics.
- Each JIRA we work should have due date and estimated time for completion
- Change JIRA status to “In Progress” when its being worked on
- On resolving the JIRA to – Resolved/Fixed, always associate a deployment ticket associated to the fix made
- If any JIRA is resolved without a deployment ticket, then the corresponding resolution should be set – eg:
Won’t fix, Duplicate, Incomplete, Cannot reproduce.
- Make sure to tick appropriate root cause checkboxes as well
Feel free to approach the core team if you have questions or concerns.
