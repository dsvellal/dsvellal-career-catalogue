RE: THANK YOU! Release 3.3.2.9 HF 04 Out the Door

You all did a bang up job in getting this release out.
There are a few things I wanted to mention and thank you around this release.
1. This release contained a lot of bug fixes that were backlogged – I believe we did over 400 bug fixes, we also
eliminated a bunch of items that were in the queue that were not bugs in the end (about 80 of them).
2. Pretty much all the work was done by the ESI team for this release (outside the API, data layer work done by
Rob) from dev to QA (ESI team did a lot of this).
3. This was a release that was not done under the gun (i.e. the customer absolutely needed this on a certain
date) – Our track record on releases of this nature was not good in the past where we end up missing the
dates or functionality. We executed on this and a lot of folks put in extra hrs to make this happen – I
appreciate us sticking to the goal.
4. There was some work done to correct some of our past sins - The date logic rework is going to pay big
dividends in the long run.
I am sure I have missed some of the other achievements – but I think Jonah said this well – when he says that he has
the confidence that we can do whatever challenges that comes up in terms of development work – because we are
laying the foundation and have such a dedicated team behind the product.
Thank You folks.
Chevy
