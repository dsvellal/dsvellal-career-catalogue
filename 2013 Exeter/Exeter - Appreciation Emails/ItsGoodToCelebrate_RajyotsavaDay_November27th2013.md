This friday, 29th of Nov 2013, between 4pm and 5pm - We are celebrating "Rajyotsava Day" here at OneGate ESI, 4th

Well.. Rajyotsava is JUST an excuse, we really want to take some time off and have some fun. So, we have lined up
exciting events and are calling every OneGate-er to take an hour off work, take part in the activities and HAVE FUN!
Apart from the impromptu events which we will announce during the day, we are posing challenges to every one of
you! DO YOU HAVE IT IN YOU?
Here are our challenges:
Challenge 1: Kannada-fy OneGate - (Product pitch event)
Pitch OneGate to the Karnataka Government official and convince them that this is the product that they NEED to
buy.
Remember, there is no restriction on the limitations of ONEGATE's capabilities (since this is hypothetical), so get
CREATIVE!
Rules:
Time limit: 3 minutes per team
Team members: No restrictions on the number of team members, as long as you have one non-kannadiga (nonkannada-speaking-colleague) in the team.

government official. NO electronic media can be used, however other forms of illustration (charts and the like) may
be used. There is two complete days for preparation so get started!
NOTE: Register your teams by sending an email with the team-name and the participants of the team to Datta
(dvellal@exeter.com)
