Dear Dattatreya Subramanya Vellal,

With the passage of another successful year 2Ol4-15 for Exeter Group, let us raise a toast to
ourselves for the year that has been!
We have delivered complex projects, with world-class quality, to our clients. We are building
engineering frameworks that will help us create top-notch software in the years to come. We
continue to nourish a culture of taking pride in our individual and collective work-products. Most
important of all, we try to do all of the above in an environment that encourages both learning and
teaching.

And we are doing all of the above in a rapidly changing global economy, of which the IT industry is
but one part. As the IT industry evolves to catch up with the new business landscape, we need to
adapt our operations accordingly. Being agile then becomes an imperative need of the day, and not
just a buzzword. The challenge for our engineering minds, in this new paradigm, now morphs into
how to deliver the same quality and value that every one of our clients has come to expect of Exeter.
This challenge, in organizations big and small, will be won or lost, at the level of the individual. The
teams which are able to marshal the creativity and well-wishes of its individual members will adopt
the organizational frameworks and - more important - adapt these for their successes. At Exeter, I
am supremely confident about our ability to take this successful path. It is in our organizational
DNA, it is in our individual abilities, and it is in our collective ambitions.
As we move into the new year, we also take great pleasure in announcing your compensation for this
year, and congratulate you on this well-deserved achievement. The affached 'Annexure: Revised
Compensation Structure' has the details of this revised compensation.

In view of the recently released Career Progression Roadmap, we also have the pleasure of providing
you the designation of 'Senior Lead - Software Development'. We wish you the very best in this
role!
Please do reach out to us for any questions or clarifications; we continue to look up to your feedback
in order to make Exeter a great place to work at and to build our careers!

Yours sincerely,
