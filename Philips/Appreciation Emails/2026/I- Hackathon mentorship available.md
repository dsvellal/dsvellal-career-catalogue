Dear Hackathon Teams,

To provide the most relevant guidance and encourage collaboration, we have reviewed and classified all submitted ideas into thematic clusters, grouping together teams working on similar or related challenges. Each cluster has been assigned a dedicated mentor. This approach allows the mentor to provide more focused support while also creating opportunities for teams to learn from one another, exchange perspectives, identify synergies, and potentially build on shared insights. Clusters are not intended to become delivery units or competition units.

Your teams have been assigned to the DICOM Interoperability cluster and Dattatreya Subramanya Vellal is available to help you:

  *   Sharpen and refine the scope of your idea
  *   Challenge assumptions and identify potential blind spots
  *   Reflect on solution design choices and architectural trade-offs
  *   Ensure alignment with broader business and technology considerations
  *   Explore alternative approaches and opportunities for greater impact

Why take advantage of this support?
Hackathons often move fast, making it tempting to jump straight into building. Having an experienced architect as a sounding board can help you focus on the most valuable problem to solve, avoid unnecessary complexity, and strengthen the overall quality of your solution. A short conversation can save significant effort later and help turn a good idea into a compelling and scalable one. The goal is not to evaluate your work, but to support your thinking, broaden perspectives, and help your team achieve the best possible outcome. Please note that the mentor is not responsible for delivering outcomes or coordinating the teams.
